# Good Morning

One morning page from everything you've connected: mail, calendar, money, whatever
you add next.

## What you get

At 07:00 a routine looks at every integration you've connected in Cabinet and writes
one page with a short section per source: what arrived in your mail, what today holds
in your calendar, what happened to your money, what moved in your repos or channels.
Each section ends in one honest line. Most of them, most days, say "nothing needs you."

The page grows with you. Connect something new and tomorrow's report simply has one
more section. Disconnect it and the section disappears, with no settings to update.

## Make it yours

Open `setup.md` in this cabinet and replace the example answers: which sources you
care about, what order you want them in, what counts as urgent for you. Plain
sentences are perfect. The routine reads it before every report, so a correction
tonight is obeyed tomorrow morning.

## How to look at it

Open the **Report** app in this cabinet. It starts pre-filled with a made-up example
so you can see the shape, and the page says plainly that it's fake. Your first real
morning takes its place.

## Connecting things

Everything connects from Integrations in Cabinet. Each app has its own page there that
says what it needs and walks you through it, and you do each one once. The report only
ever includes sources that are actually connected, and its last section always says in
one line what it looked at and what it couldn't reach, so a quiet report is never hiding
a broken connection.

## What's inside

- **Morning Reporter**: the one agent. Checks what's connected, writes the page.
- **Morning Report**: the one routine. Runs at 07:00 every day.
- **Report**: the one page. Opens on today, with a dropdown for earlier days.
- **setup.md**: your answers. The report is generic until this makes it yours.
