# Setup

Change these here, or tell the Pipeline Reader in the chat.

- **Your Salesforce address:** (empty until you add it, for example https://yourcompany.my.salesforce.com)
- **Company name:** (empty: the page says Your pipeline)
- **Currency:** USD
- **A deal has gone quiet after:** 14 days without a call, email or meeting

Your Salesforce address is the one in your browser when you open Salesforce, up to
`.my.salesforce.com` (or `.lightning.force.com`). With it, every deal on the page opens in
Salesforce.
