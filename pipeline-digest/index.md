# Pipeline Digest

Every morning, the deals that moved in Salesforce, what closes this week, what went quiet, and
what is due today.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your pipeline**.

## What you get

- **The morning in one line**: your open pipeline, how many deals moved since yesterday, what
  closes this week and how many tasks are late, with a small chart of new deals over the last
  28 days.
- **Moved since yesterday**: deals that changed stage, amount or close date, and the ones won
  or lost.
- **Closing this week**: deals set to close in the next 7 days, each with its next step, and
  the ones whose close date already passed.
- **Gone quiet**: open deals with no call, email or meeting for 14 days or more (you choose
  the number in Setup), the biggest first.
- **Tasks due today**: your tasks due today and the late ones.
- **New leads**: who came in since yesterday, from where.
- **Pipeline numbers**: new deals, won, lost and the win rate for 7 or 28 days against the
  days before, your open deals by stage and where new leads came from.
- **What changed since last week**: the few things that moved most, in plain words.
- **Ideas**: a follow-up for the quietest deal, today's call list, a close plan, first emails
  to new leads, a weekly summary page, a month-end forecast note. Ask once, switch one on to
  run on a schedule, or keep it on the page as a box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** Every deal, amount and date comes from Salesforce as it answers, as the
person who connected it sees it. The page never guesses.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect Salesforce** and sign in with your Salesforce account. Your
   Salesforce admin may need to approve Cabinet once.
2. Open **Setup** and add your Salesforce address, so every deal opens in Salesforce. Or tell
   the Pipeline Reader in the chat.
3. A few minutes later your own pipeline is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up company, and says so.

Salesforce opens its API on the Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance and Developer editions, and
on Professional with the API add-on. Your Salesforce admin can tell you which one you have.

## It reads, and changes only what you say yes to

The morning catch-up only reads. Your team adds a task, adds a note, or changes one field on
one record only when you ask in the chat and only after you see the exact change and say yes.
A routine you set up may change only exactly what you asked for and approved. Cabinet never
deletes a record, never changes many at once, and nothing on the page changes Salesforce.

## What's inside

- **Pipeline Reader** is the one agent. Reads your Salesforce and writes the page.
- **Morning Pipeline Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your pipeline** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
- **Setup** holds your Salesforce address, your company's name, the currency and when a deal
  counts as gone quiet.
