# Board Desk

Your Trello boards, lists and cards in one place. The Board Desk app shows what is due,
what moved, and which cards may be stalled. Cabinet asks before it comments, creates, moves
or archives a card. It never deletes a Trello card.

The first view uses labelled example rows. After the Morning board routine writes its first
summary, your own cards replace them.

## Routines

- **Morning board** runs weekdays at 08:00. It reads Trello and writes `boards/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
  It does not change Trello.
- The Board Reader agent prepares that page. Agent actions in chat ask for your approval.

Use the seven Trello actions in Cabinet chat: summarize a board, check due cards, draft a
recap, comment, create, move or archive. The last four ask you before they write to Trello.
