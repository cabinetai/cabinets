# TikTok Digest

Every morning, how your recent TikTok videos did, which one moved most, and three ideas for
the next one.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your TikTok**.

## What you get

- **How your videos did**: views, likes, comments and shares for each recent video, what
  changed since yesterday, which one moved most and which one was quietest.
- **Account numbers**: your followers and likes, and what your videos from the last 7 and 28
  days gathered between them.
- **Ideas for your next videos**: three ideas a day, each one backed by a video that did well.
- **Ask about your account**: what your best videos have in common, which day you do best,
  how this month compares with the last. The answer comes up in the chat.
- **Ideas**: a follow-up to the video that moved most, a new caption for a quiet one, a
  weekly summary. Ask once, switch one on to run on a schedule, or keep it on the page as a
  box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** TikTok shares your account's counts and each video's totals with apps
like Cabinet. It does not share what comments say, daily views, watch time or where viewers
come from. What changed since yesterday, or over 7 and 28 days, is counted from Cabinet's own
morning reads, so those numbers fill in as the days go by. The page says what it has and
never guesses.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect TikTok** and sign in to TikTok. It takes about a minute, once.
2. A few minutes later your own account is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up account, and says so.

## It only reads

Cabinet's TikTok can only read: your profile, its counts, and your own videos with their
numbers. It never posts, uploads, comments, deletes or changes anything on TikTok. When you
ask for a caption or a plan, you get a draft in the chat to copy.

## What's inside

- **Account Reader** is the one agent. Reads your account and writes the page.
- **Morning TikTok Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your TikTok** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
