# OneDrive for Personal

Your own OneDrive, sorted. Every morning at 7 it reads your OneDrive and shows:

- **Shared with you**: what family, friends and school shared with you.
- **Where you left off**: the files you worked on lately, one tap away.
- **What fills your OneDrive**: your storage and the biggest files in it, before it fills up.

On Sundays it checks **what is open to anyone** with the link, the private files first. On the
1st of the month it finds your **important papers** (passport, ID, insurance, the house, the
car, taxes) and the **dates** in them: what expires, renews or ends.

Then you can just ask. "Where is my passport scan?" "When does the car insurance renew?" The
answer names the file and where it is.

## To start

Open **My files** and press **Connect OneDrive**. You sign in with Microsoft, once. Until
then the page shows a made-up person, clearly marked.

For now OneDrive works in the Cabinet app on your computer.

## It only reads

My files never changes, moves, shares or deletes a file in OneDrive. When something needs
fixing (a passport open to anyone, a OneDrive almost full), it shows you where, and you do it
in OneDrive.

## Inside

- **My files**: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about you.
- **Reports**: one page per morning, the Sunday sharing check, the monthly papers, and the
  lists you ask for, like what expires this year.
