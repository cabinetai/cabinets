# Issue Desk

Your Linear at a glance, every working morning: the issues on you, what is due this week,
what waits in triage, what changed since yesterday, and how each team's cycle is going.

## What you get

- **The desk**: the app this cabinet opens on. Your current cycles on a dark sheet at the
  top, four numbers (your open issues, due this week, in triage, cycle done), then lists of
  your issues, what is due, triage, what was updated lately and your projects. Every row
  opens the issue in Linear.
- **A stand-up, written for you**: at 08:30 on weekdays the Issue Keeper writes
  `standup/<date>.md`. What moved yesterday, what is due, and what is stuck, in a few lines
  you can paste into your team's channel.
- **Ask it anything**: "What is on my plate?", "What changed since yesterday?", or describe a
  bug and it drafts the issue for you.

## To start

Connect Linear from the desk, or from Integrations in Cabinet. Cabinet keeps a copy of your
Linear workspace, one page per issue, project and cycle, and the Issue Keeper reads that
copy. Until then the desk shows a made-up team, clearly marked as an example.

## What it changes in Linear

Nothing on its own. The Issue Keeper reads the copy and never writes to Linear. When you ask
for a new issue it drafts the title, the description, the team and the priority in the chat,
and the issue is made only when you press **New issue** in Cabinet's Linear page.

## Inside

- **Issue Keeper**: the one agent. Reads the copy, writes the stand-up, answers questions.
- **Morning stand-up**: the one routine. Runs at 08:30, Monday to Friday.
- **Issue desk**: the app, with `DATA.md` describing the files it reads.

Cabinet is not affiliated with Linear.
