# Brief

**Asked for:** "A page for my kid's lemonade stand — when, where, prices, and it
should feel sunny." (This is the studio's built-in example; the brief is invented.)

**Decisions made:** Weekend-morning hours phrased the way the stand actually works
("until the jug runs out") rather than a fake closing time. Prices as a dashed-line
menu because that reads at a glance. All art drawn inline so the page needs nothing
from the internet.

**Edits since:** none.
