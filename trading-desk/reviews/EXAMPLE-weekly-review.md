# Weekly review

## What the numbers say

You closed 14 trades. The trades where you followed your plan made money as a group. The trades you marked with a mistake lost money as a group. Your method is not the problem. The exceptions are.

## Keep

- **Planned breakouts and breakdowns.** They are your winners, and you sized every one of them by the stop.
- **Skipping a trade after two losses.** You did it once this month and it saved you a third.

## Stop

- **Moving your stop.** One trade cost more than two full losses because the stop moved when the price got close.
- **Trading right after a loss.** The trade tagged "Revenge trade" was entered minutes after a stop. It had no plan.

## Try this week

Write the plan before the open, not during it. If a trade is not in Plans by the bell, it waits until tomorrow.
