# What I know about my trading

This page is the desk's memory of you. Every assistant reads it before it answers you,
checks a plan or writes a review, so it does not start from nothing each time.

## My rules

Yours, in your own words. Each line here also appears on the desk as a box to tick before
you take a trade. The assistants quote them back to you and never change them.

- I do not trade in the first 15 minutes after the open.
- I take no more than three trades a day.
- I do not add to a losing trade.

## What my numbers show

The Risk Coach keeps this part each Friday, from your closed trades. Each line carries its
number and the weeks it covers, and a line goes when newer trades no longer support it.

Nothing yet. It starts after your first week of closed trades.

## What I am working on

One or two things you want to get better at. The Coach checks them in each weekly review.
