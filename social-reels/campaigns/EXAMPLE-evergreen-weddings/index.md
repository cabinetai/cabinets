# EXAMPLE — Evergreen Weddings

**This campaign is made up.** It ships with the cabinet so the dashboard has something
to show before you have made anything, and the clips are plain colour cards, not real
video. Your first real campaign replaces it.

A short reel for a wedding venue: the grounds, the party, and a line asking people to
book a viewing. Three shots, vertical, about five seconds each.

Look at how the three shots differ. One is approved. One was rejected and shot again.
One has two takes waiting for someone to pick between them. That is the normal state of
a campaign — most generated video is not good, and the job is choosing, not collecting.
