# Reel Campaigns

Generate social reels a shot at a time, then keep the takes worth keeping.

## What you get

A campaign is a folder. Inside it, a reel is a list of shots, and each shot is one short
vertical clip. You write what the shot should show, press Regenerate, and a clip appears.
Most of them will not be right. That is normal, and it is what the dashboard is for.

Every shot keeps every take you paid for. Approving, rejecting and switching between
takes costs nothing and happens instantly, and all of it is recorded in this cabinet's
history, so nothing you click is lost.

## Nothing runs on a timer

There is no weekly routine here and there never will be. Video is expensive enough that
every single clip should be something a person asked for, on purpose, by clicking. The
cabinet generates when you tell it to and at no other time.

## How to look at it

Open the **Campaigns** app. It comes with a made-up campaign for a wedding company so the
page is not empty — those clips are plain colour cards, not real video, and the page says
so. Approve and Reject work on it straight away. Your first real campaign takes its place.

## Connect Higgsfield

1. In Cabinet, open **Integrations**, find **Higgsfield**, and click **Connect**.
2. Click **Open Higgsfield sign-in**. Higgsfield opens in a new tab.
3. Sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft, or email, then approve access.

There is nothing to paste.

Sign in with the Higgsfield account that holds your credits. A different login creates a
brand-new free account, and your clips and credits end up there instead.

The sign-in link expires after about five minutes. If it fails, click **Open Higgsfield
sign-in** again for a fresh one.
