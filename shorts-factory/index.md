# Shorts Factory

Short vertical videos that market your business, made from what your cabinet already knows.
No editing skills needed: pick an idea, and the script, the captions and the finished video
are made for you, ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

It goes in six steps, all in the Studio, and the top bar always shows where you are:

- **Brand**: what every video is built on. What you offer, who it's for, why you, your goal,
  how you sound, where you post and what never to say. **Fill from my pages** reads your
  cabinet and fills each part, with the page it came from. Anything it cannot find waits for
  you, so nothing is made up.
- **Idea**: short video ideas, each from something true about your business. Pick one.
- **Script**: the scenes, each with the words on screen, what is said and the picture.
  Change, add, move or remove any scene. Nothing is made yet.
- **Look**: how the captions look. Pick a style, a font, colours and a place, or drag the
  caption in the preview. Click a word to colour it.
- **Make**: a picture for each scene, a voice that reads it, then the video. They come from
  Cabinet's Images and Voiceovers integrations, so the first video asks you to connect them.
  Want movement? Choose the first scene or every scene to move, and Cabinet's Video
  integration turns those pictures into short clips. The price shows before you make it.
- **Post**: watch it, download it, and copy the text for each platform.

Your profile lives in [profile.md](profile.md). Edit it there or in the Studio.
