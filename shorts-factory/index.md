# Shorts Factory

Short vertical videos that market your business, made from what your cabinet already knows.
No editing skills needed: pick an idea, and the script, the captions and the finished video
are made for you, ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

It works in three parts, all in the Studio:

- **Profile**: what every video is built on. What you offer, who it's for, why you, your
  goal, how you sound, where you post and what never to say. **Fill in my profile** reads
  your cabinet and fills each part, with the page it came from. Anything it cannot find
  waits for you, so nothing is made up.
- **Ideas**: short video ideas, each from something true about your business. Pick one.
- **Videos**: the script, scene by scene, with the words on screen, what is said and the
  picture. Change any of it, then make the video and download it.

Your profile lives in [profile.md](profile.md). Edit it there or in the Studio.
