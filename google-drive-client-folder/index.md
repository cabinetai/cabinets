# Client Folder

One client or project folder in Drive, with a page on top of it that stays current.

## What you get

**The summary** is one page that says everything you would want to know before a call:
who this is, what was agreed, what it is worth, what has been delivered, where it stands.
It is rewritten every morning from the folder, so it is never a document somebody forgot
to update. It is the folder, read for you.

**Open items** is the short list of things still waiting on someone. Written every Friday
afternoon, oldest first, with who owes what. It is the page you read before you go home.

You can also just ask. "What did we agree about revisions?" "When does this renew?" "What
have we invoiced?" The answers come out of the contract and the emails and the notes that
are already in the folder.

## How to look at it

The Drive folder sits in the sidebar of this cabinet. Open it and you are looking at the
real files, the same ones the Drive app keeps on this computer. Nothing is copied
anywhere and nothing in Drive is changed.

**The summary** and **Open items** are pages in this cabinet. They come pre-filled with a
made-up client so you can see the shape straight away. Both say clearly that the client
is invented. Your first real run replaces them.

## One folder, one cabinet

This template is built for one folder: one client, one project, one deal. That is what
makes the summary worth reading, because every sentence in it is about the same thing.

For a second client, install it again. Two cabinets named after two clients read better
than one cabinet that is about both.

## Before it can read your real folder

Connect Google Drive when Cabinet asks, or later from Integrations, and pick the client's
folder. You do it once. It uses the Google Drive app on this computer, so nothing new
gets installed and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your team reads. It does not write to Drive, rename anything, or move a file. Until you
connect a folder, both pages keep showing the example and the two routines wait.

## If the pages still show the example

The routines only write when they can actually read a folder. If one has run and the
example is still sitting there, no Drive folder is in this cabinet yet. Nothing was
overwritten and nothing errored. Add the folder and run it again.

## What is inside

- **Account Reader**, the one person. Reads the folder, keeps both pages honest.
- **Refresh Client Summary**, every morning at 07:30. **Friday Open Items**, Fridays at
  16:00.
- **The summary** and **Open items**, the two pages above.
