# Calendar for Creators

Your shoot week, from your Google Calendar. Every morning at 7 it reads your calendar and
lays out:

- **This week's posts**: everything going live and every draft that is due, day by day,
  with the brand for each one.
- **Shoot days**: every shoot coming up, with sunrise, sunset and golden hour at that place
  on that day, and a word when your shoot misses the good light.
- **Clashes**: two things at once, a post on a day you travel, or two brands going live the
  same day.
- **From your brand deals**: with Gmail connected too, the dates brands gave you in their
  emails (shoot, draft, go live), and whether each one is on your calendar. One tap puts
  them there, after your yes.

On top, your week as a strip of film: one frame a day, so you see the whole week at once.

## To start

Open the **Shoot week** and press **Connect Google Calendar**. Until then it shows an
example week of a travel creator, clearly marked.

It works best when your events say what they are: "Masada shoot", "Monsoon reel goes live",
"Script due". Your shoot planner learns the rest from your calendar.

## It asks first

The morning read never changes your calendar. An event is added only when you ask in the
chat, after it shows you each one and you say yes.
