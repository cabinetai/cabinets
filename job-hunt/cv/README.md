# Your CV and letters

Drop your current CV here — PDF, Word document or plain text, whatever you have.
Cover letters too, if you keep them.

When you ask the clerk to tailor your CV for an application or draft a cover letter,
it works from the files in this folder and saves the draft back here under a new name
— `cv-riverline.md`, `cover-riverline.md` — so your originals are never touched.
