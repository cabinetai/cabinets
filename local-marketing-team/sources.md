# Event Sources

> Example sources for Austin. Replace with the calendars, venues and teams near your own locations. The Local Researcher checks these in order and records the source URL on every event file.

Check sports first, because game days are the most reliable traffic driver. Then concerts and festivals, then holidays and community events, then weather.

## Sports (check daily)
| Team or venue | What to check | Near |
|---|---|---|
| Texas Longhorns football, Darrell K Royal Stadium | Home schedule, kickoff times (often set 6 to 12 days ahead), TV network | north-loop |
| Austin FC, Q2 Stadium | Home matches, kickoff times | north-loop |
| Texas Longhorns basketball, Moody Center | Home games (Nov to Mar) | north-loop |
| Round Rock Express (Apr to Sep) | Weekend home games | (regional) |
| Big national games (NFL, playoffs, World Series) | Watch-party nights, any location | all |

Search for each team's official schedule page. Record kickoff time as "TBA" until it is announced.

## Concerts and festivals (check weekly)
| Source | What to check | Near |
|---|---|---|
| ACL Music Festival, Zilker Park | Dates, set times, shuttle and road closures | south-lamar |
| Moody Center, Stubb's, Auditorium Shores, Palmer Events Center | Large shows (5,000+ capacity) | south-lamar, north-loop |
| City of Austin special events calendar, park and street closure notices | Festivals, races, parades | all |
| Visit Austin events calendar, local event listings and alt-weeklies | Festivals, markets, cultural events | all |

## Holidays and community (check monthly)
- National and cultural holidays: Día de los Muertos (Nov 1 to 2), Thanksgiving, Cinco de Mayo, Juneteenth, back-to-school, graduation weekends.
- Neighborhood events: East Austin art studio tours, farmers markets, school fundraisers.

## Weather (check daily, 7 days out)
- National Weather Service forecast for Austin. Flag rain days and days with a high under 50°F (triggers `rainy-day-consomme`). Flag extreme heat (over 100°F) for cold-drink messaging.

## Rules
- Prefer the organizer's own page. Use listings only to discover events, then confirm on the organizer's page.
- Record `dates_verified: false` until you confirm dates on an official page.
- Skip events under 1,000 expected attendance unless they are within half a mile of a location.
