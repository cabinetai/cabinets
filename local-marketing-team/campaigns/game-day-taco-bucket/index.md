# Game Day Taco Bucket, Longhorns home game

*Event date, opponent and kickoff time are illustrative. Verify on the official schedule before anything is posted.*

## Goal
Sell out the 40-bucket cap at North Loop for the home game, with at least 30 buckets pre-ordered by Friday 20:00.

## Audience
**Game day groups** (primary): people hosting watch parties in Hyde Park and North Loop. **Students** (secondary): splitting a bucket six ways is cheaper than going out.

## Offer (exactly as in the promotion library)
24 tacos, chips and 3 salsas for **$64**. Order ahead, ready on the North Loop pickup shelf two hours before kickoff. Pre-order by 20:00 the night before. Pickup only.

## Big idea
**"Feed the whole watch party before kickoff."** The bucket is the easy win for whoever is hosting. We show it going from the tortilla press to the pickup shelf to a living room full of fans in burnt orange.

## Key message
One bucket feeds six to eight. Order by Friday night, grab it from the shelf, be home before kickoff.
