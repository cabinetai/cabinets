# Late Night Al Pastor, ACL weekend two

*Festival dates are illustrative. Verify on the festival's official site before anything is posted.*

## Goal
Sell 150 Late Night Four Packs at the South Lamar walk-up window across Friday and Saturday night (21:00 to 02:00), plus 80 on Sunday until 23:00.

## Audience
**Late night crowd**: festival-goers walking out of Zilker Park after the last set, hungry, on foot, phones in hand.

## Offer (exactly as in the promotion library)
**Late Night Four Pack**: 4 al pastor tacos and chips for **$16** after 21:00. Show a same-day event wristband for a free horchata (one per wristband). Walk-up window and counter only.

## Big idea
**"After the last set, follow the trompo."** The trompo glowing in the window is the beacon on the walk home. Everything points people from the park to the window in under ten minutes.
