# Learnings

What worked, what did not, and what to try next. The Marketing Lead adds to this page after each campaign, and the weekly performance job summarizes live campaigns here. Promotion matching reads this page before scoring.

> The entries below are illustrative examples so the page is not empty. Replace them with real results.

## Standing lessons
- **Announce game-day offers by Thursday.** Bucket pre-orders double when the post goes out two days before kickoff instead of the morning of.
- **Name the neighborhood.** Posts that say "North Loop" or "South Lamar" in the first line get more saves than generic ones.
- **Stories beat feed posts for same-night offers.** Late-night offers convert from Stories with a location sticker, posted after 19:00.
- **Do not lead with price.** Food close-ups with the price on the last frame outperform price-first graphics.
