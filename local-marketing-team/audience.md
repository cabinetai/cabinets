# Audience

> Example segments. Replace with your own. Campaign briefs must name one primary segment from this list.

## Game day groups
Hosting a watch party or tailgating before a home game. They order buckets and trays, usually one to three hours before kickoff, and they plan the day before. Reach them Thursday to Saturday morning.

## Late night crowd
Leaving a show, a festival or a shift. They decide in the moment and walk or scoot to whatever is open and close. Reach them with Stories in the evening and pinned location info.

## Students
Phone first, value focused, travel in groups. Respond to visible prices and anything they can share with friends.

## Neighborhood regulars
Loyal and local. Respond to community events, cultural holidays and anything that celebrates East Austin.

## On Instagram
- Most engagement between 11:00 and 13:00 and between 18:00 and 21:00.
- Reels drive reach; carousels drive saves; Stories drive same-day visits.
- Followers are about 70% Austin metro, 60% aged 21 to 34.
