# Business

Los Tacos is a fictional fast-casual taqueria with three locations in Austin, Texas. Tortillas are pressed to order, the al pastor is cooked on a trompo, and every location stays open late on weekends. Most guests order at the counter or online for pickup; catering trays and taco buckets are a growing share of weekend sales.

## What we sell

- Tacos, burritos and bowls built on a short menu (see [[menu]]).
- Group orders: taco buckets, catering trays, party packs.
- Drinks: aguas frescas, Mexican sodas, beer and margaritas at the dine-in locations.

## How we make money

- **Dine-in and counter** about 55% of sales, strongest from 11:00 to 14:00 and 18:00 to 21:00.
- **Pickup and delivery** about 35%, peaks on game days and weekend evenings.
- **Catering and buckets** about 10%, but the highest average order value (around $60 to $120).

## Marketing goals this season

1. Grow group orders (buckets and trays) on game days and event weekends.
2. Fill the 21:00 to 01:00 late-night window at South Lamar and North Loop.
3. Build a local following on Instagram: more saves and shares, more people who tag a location.
4. Keep every promotion profitable. No discount deeper than the limits in [[promotions]].

## What we will not do

- No paid partnership claims with teams, leagues, festivals or venues we are not partnered with. Do not use their logos or trademarks in creative.
- No alcohol promotions aimed at under-21 audiences, and no "drink specials" language in paid posts.
- No price claims that are not in [[menu]] or [[promotions]].
