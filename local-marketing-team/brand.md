# Brand

> Example brand guide. Replace with your own. The Social Content Creator follows this page for every caption, script and story.

## Personality
Los Tacos sounds like the friend who knows where to eat after the game. Warm, a little playful, proud of the food, and always local. We talk about the tortilla, the trompo and the neighborhood, not about "deals".

## Voice rules
- Lead with the food or the moment, then the offer.
- Short sentences. One idea per line in captions.
- Spanish words we already use on the menu are fine (trompo, consommé, aguas frescas). Do not force Spanglish.
- Say the neighborhood name ("North Loop", "South Lamar"). Locals care which one.
- Never punch down at rival teams, other restaurants or neighborhoods. Friendly rivalry only.

## Visual style
- Real food, real counters, real people. Close-ups of the tortilla press and the trompo.
- Warm light, terracotta and marigold accents, cream backgrounds for text cards.
- Text on images: two lines maximum, bold sans, high contrast.
- Never use team, league, festival or venue logos. Use colors and generic props (a foam finger, a wristband) instead of marks.

## Words we like
hand-pressed, trompo, fresh off the comal, bucket, neighbors, late night, fuel up, see you after

## Words we avoid
cheap, best in Austin (unverifiable), official, exclusive partner, insane, game-changer
