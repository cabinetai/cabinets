# Your setup

Replace the answers below with your own. Plain sentences are perfect — the teller
reads this before every morning page.

**What deserves a row:** Any failed payment, any dispute, any refund, and any single
payment over $150. (Below that, routine payments fold into the headline count.)

**Customers I watch:** The annual-plan customers — a wobble there matters more than
ten small ones. (Name names if you like; the teller will use them.)

**How I think about a bad day:** One failed card is Tuesday; three is a pattern I
want called out plainly in the lead.

**Tone:** Plain words, no dashboard-speak. "Money came in" beats "revenue was
realized."
