# monday.com Tasks

Every morning, the monday.com items that are actually on you today.

## What you get

One page. Every item with your name on it gets one line — what it is, which board it lives
on, where it stands, and the one thing that happens next. The item somebody else is stuck
behind sits at the top, because that one costs other people their morning too. The thing
you are only waiting on a reply for sits at the bottom.

**Your work, not the whole board.** Not a report a manager runs: it is the items assigned
to you, plus anything where you are the reason someone else cannot move, pulled from every
board you are on — so the quiet board you forgot about still turns up when it needs you.

Boards name their own statuses and no two teams name them alike. Whatever your team calls
it — "Working on it", "Stuck", "Waiting for review" — the page sorts it into five plain
words, and an item too thin to judge is marked unclear rather than guessed at.

The morning routine only reads. It never creates an item, changes a status or posts an
update on your boards.

## How to look at it

Open the **My Items** app in this cabinet. It comes pre-filled with a made-up example so
you can see the shape of it straight away — the page says clearly that those items are
fake. Your first real list takes its place.

## Before it can read your real boards

Connect monday.com in Cabinet's integrations screen. It is one step and you paste nothing:
click Connect, sign in with monday.com in the browser tab that opens, and approve it.

On that approval screen monday.com shows a red "Unverified source" notice. That is normal
for this connection and not a sign anything is wrong — click Authorize and carry on.

Until you connect, the page keeps showing the example.

## What's inside

- **Item Reader** — the one agent. Reads your items, writes the list.
- **Morning monday.com Tasks** — the one routine. Runs at 08:00 every day.
- **My Items** — the one page. Opens on today, with a dropdown for earlier days.
