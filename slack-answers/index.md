# Slack Team Memory

Every question your team has already answered in Slack, kept as a page in your knowledge base so nobody has to ask it again.

When someone new asks "where do I get X?", the answer is already here, with its source.
Search it, find it, copy it. The cabinet opens on it: **Answer Book**.

## What you get

- **One page per question**: the answer in a few plain lines, the other ways people asked
  it, and a link to every thread it came from. The pages are ordinary pages in
  **answers/topics**, so Cabinet's search finds them and every other agent can read them.
- **How often it was asked**: a question asked four times is a gap in what people read. The
  one that keeps coming back is marked worth pinning.
- **Settled, Shifting or Open**: when an answer changes, the page is rewritten and keeps what
  it used to be. A question nobody answered stays Open.
- **Changed in the last sweep**: what is new since last night, with a log of every sweep.
- **Ask about any answer**: open an answer for **Draft a reply with this answer**, **Turn
  this into a doc page** or **This answer is wrong**, and on an Open one **Look for an
  answer**. Each one goes to the Answer Keeper in the chat beside the page.
- **Put the top answers in Slack**: one press, and the Answer Keeper drafts a Team FAQ canvas
  from the most asked Settled answers, for a channel you name.
- **Ideas and Layout**: questions to try, and the parts of the page you want to see, just
  for you. The box at the bottom takes your own question.

Its value grows with time, so nothing is thrown away. A note you add to a page under its own
heading is kept. If an answer is a key or a password, the page says where it is kept, never
the secret itself.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect Slack**, check the workspace on Slack's page and press
   **Allow**. Cabinet then asks which channels it may use. Every public channel is in by
   default. A private channel, like a support channel, is in when you add it by name.
2. Your first sweep starts right away. It reads the last 7 days and tells you what it finds
   as it goes, a few minutes in all. After that it runs every night at 2.

Until then, the page shows a made-up team, and says so.

## It reads, and writes only after your yes

The sweep only reads. Nothing reaches Slack on a schedule. Setting a channel to **Write** on
Cabinet's channel screen allows a write there, and does not start one. Two things can write
to Slack, and both happen only in the chat, after you say yes to the exact words, in a
channel set to Write:

- **A reply you approved**: posted once, in that thread.
- **The Team FAQ canvas**: created or updated in the channel you named.

On Read you get the text to copy, and nothing is written. Direct messages are never read.
Anything posted or made shows you as its author, because Cabinet signs in to Slack as you.

## What's inside

- **Answer Book** is the page the cabinet opens on.
- **Answer Keeper** is the one agent. Reads the threads and keeps one page per question.
- **Nightly Answer Sweep** is the one routine. Runs every night at 2.
