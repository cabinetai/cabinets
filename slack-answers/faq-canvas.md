# FAQ Canvas

Every Monday at 08:00 the Answer Keeper can keep a canvas called **Team FAQ** in one Slack
channel: the answers your team asks for most, each with a link to the thread it came from,
bookmarked at the top of the channel so nobody has to search for it.

It is off until you choose the channel.

## Turn it on

1. Write the channel's name after `channel:` at the top of this page, for example
   `channel: team-questions`. The `#` is optional.
2. On Cabinet's Slack channel screen, set that channel to **Write**. On **Read**, the
   routine leaves Slack alone and says why here.

Leave the other lines empty. The routine fills them in: `canvas` is the canvas it made, so
it edits the same one every week instead of making a new one.

## Good to know

- The canvas is made by your Slack account, because that is how Cabinet signs in. It
  shows you as its author.
- Only answers marked Settled go in, most asked first, at most 20.
- An answer learned in a private channel is left out, unless the canvas lives in that same
  channel.
- Edits made inside the canvas are replaced on Monday. To fix an answer, answer the
  question again in Slack. The next night's sweep picks it up.
- Nothing is ever posted as a message.
- To move the canvas, change `channel`. A new canvas is made there; the old one stays
  where it was until you delete it in Slack.
- To turn it off, empty `channel`. The canvas stays in Slack as it was.
