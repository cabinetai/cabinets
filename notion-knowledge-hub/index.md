# Knowledge Hub

A wiki is only as good as its freshest page. This keeps your Notion wiki honest.

Cabinet keeps a copy of the Notion pages you picked, up to date on its own. This cabinet
opens on one home that Cabinet draws from that copy:

- **The one answer**: how many pages changed this week and how many need a review, with
  Ask the wiki, Find a page and Review stale pages a press away.
- **Four numbers**: pages, edited this week, need a review, broken links.
- **Recently edited**, **New this week**, **Needs a review** and **Broken links**, each row
  a page you can open.
- **Top editors**: who keeps the wiki alive this month.
- **The latest brief**: this morning's note from the Knowledge Curator.
- **Tools**: what changed this week, find a page, summarize a page, stale pages, draft an
  FAQ. One press each; the answer opens in the chat beside the page.
- **Ask**: anything in your pages, in your words.

Until the first brief, the home shows example rows, clearly marked. Your own pages replace
them.

## To start

Connect Notion from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Notion asks which pages
to share; pick your wiki or team space. The first copy takes a minute or two.

## It only reads

Nothing here changes a page in Notion. The Knowledge Curator reads the copy and writes its
briefs into `hub/` in this cabinet, nowhere else.

## Inside

- **Knowledge Curator**: the agent. Reads your Notion pages and answers in the chat.
- **Morning knowledge brief**: the routine, every weekday at 8. Writes one short note on
  what changed, what is new, what needs a review, which links broke, and what the wiki
  could not answer.
- **Briefs**: one page per morning in `hub/`.
