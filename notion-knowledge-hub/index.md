# Knowledge Hub

A wiki is only as good as its freshest page. This keeps your Notion wiki honest.

Cabinet keeps a copy of the Notion pages you picked, up to date on its own. This cabinet
opens on **Wiki**, a page made from that copy every morning:

- **The one answer**: how many pages changed this week and how many need a review.
- **Three numbers**: pages, edited this week, need a review.
- **Recently edited**: the newest edits and who made them, each page a link to Notion.
- **Needs a review**: pages nobody edited for 90 days that other pages still link to.
- **Most linked**: the pages the rest of the wiki leans on.
- **Who edits**: who edited the most pages in the last 30 days.

Press **Update now** to read your Notion again. Until the first reading, the page shows a
made-up team, clearly marked.

## To start

Connect Notion from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Notion asks which pages
to share; pick your wiki or team space. The first copy takes a minute or two.

## It only reads

Nothing here changes a page in Notion. The Knowledge Curator reads the copy and writes its
briefs into `hub/` and the page's data into `wiki/data/` in this cabinet, nowhere else.

## Inside

- **Knowledge Curator**: the agent. Reads your Notion pages and answers in the chat.
- **Wiki**: the page this cabinet opens on.
- **Morning knowledge brief**: the routine, every weekday at 8. Updates Wiki and writes one
  short note on what changed, what is new, what needs a review, which links broke, and what
  the wiki could not answer.
- **Briefs**: one page per morning in `hub/`.
