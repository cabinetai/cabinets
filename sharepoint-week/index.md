# SharePoint Week

Every Monday, what your team changed in SharePoint while you weren't looking.

## What you get

One page, waiting for you at 8 on Monday morning. Every document somebody else touched in
your team sites last week gets one line — the file, the site it sits in, who changed it and
how, and then the thing you came for: whether it touches anything of yours. The ones
nearest your own work sit at the top; a file that changed six times is not automatically a
file that matters.

Your own edits are left out. You know what you did last week; this is the part you missed.

The routine only reads: it never edits, renames, moves or shares a document, and never
mails or posts to Teams about one. It never quotes what is inside a document either — it
says a file changed and why that might matter to you, not what it says.

## Not the same as Microsoft 365 Brief

If you have its sibling **Microsoft 365 Brief** as well: that one runs every morning and
tells you what today asks of you across mail, meetings and messages, while this one runs
once a week and tells you what other people did to your documents.

## How to look at it

Open the **Week in Files** app in this cabinet. It comes pre-filled with a made-up example
so you can see the shape of it straight away — the page says clearly that those files are
fake. Your first real Monday takes its place. Some Mondays it will be short, or empty; a
quiet week is a real answer, not a broken page.

## Before it can read your real sites

Connect Microsoft 365 in Cabinet's integrations screen — SharePoint has no sign-in of its
own, and the same one covers Outlook, Teams and OneDrive too. It is an ordinary Microsoft
sign-in; on a company account your IT department may have to allow it first, and the screen
tells you what to hand them if they ask. Until you connect, the page keeps the example.

## What's inside

- **Site Watcher** — the one agent. Reads last week's changes, writes the page.
- **Weekly SharePoint Changes** — the one routine. Runs at 08:00 every Monday.
- **Week in Files** — the one page. Opens on the latest week, with a dropdown for earlier ones.
