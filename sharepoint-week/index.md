# SharePoint Week

Every Monday at 8, a ledger of what your team changed in SharePoint last week: price
lists, contracts, quotes, procedures. Who changed which file, in which folder, with a
button to open each one. Open **Week in Files** to see it.

## Ideas to try

Ask what changed in your price list, which contracts were edited this month, or which
files nobody touched in a year. Any idea can run on its own every week, once you approve
it in the chat.

## Before the first Monday

Connect SharePoint on this computer. Cabinet shows you each step: get the OneDrive app,
add your team's folder in SharePoint, then pick it. It takes a few minutes. Until then,
Week in Files shows a made-up example.

## It only reads

Site Watcher looks at file dates and names. It never changes, moves or deletes anything
in SharePoint, and the weekly page never copies what a document says.

## What is inside

- **Week in Files**: this week's ledger, and ideas for your business.
- **Past weeks**: one page for every weekly look.
- **Site Watcher**: the teammate who looks every Monday and answers your questions.
