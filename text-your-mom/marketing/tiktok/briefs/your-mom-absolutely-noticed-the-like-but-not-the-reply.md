# Your mom absolutely noticed the like but not the reply

**Format:** Talking head, 15 to 20 seconds
**Hook:** You liked the photo but did not answer the text

## Script

1. To camera, flat: "You liked the photo but did not answer the text."
2. "So now she knows two things. You have your phone. And you saw her."
3. Hold up the phone. Her text is three days old. Her dog photo has your heart on it.
4. "You did not ignore her. You just gave her a receipt."

## Call To Action

"Text your mom. She already knows you are awake."

## Notes

Keep the guilt on the person talking, never on the mom. The product stays out of it until the last line.
