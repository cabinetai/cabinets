# TikTok Growth

This imported child cabinet turns the emotional tension behind Text Your Mom into funny, swipeable, high-volume short-form creative.

## Positioning

The tone is not "be a better daughter." The tone is "you absolutely meant to reply three days ago and now you are pretending the notification does not exist."

## Team

- Trend Scout
- Script Writer
- Image Creator
- Post Optimizer

## Core Assets

- `content-ideas.csv` for the backlog and the post schedule
- `competitors.csv` for adjacent apps and content styles
- `briefs/` for weekly campaign framing
- `analytics/` for performance notes
- `content-calendar/` for planning the week: Monday to Sunday with real dates and visible gaps, the unscheduled ideas by priority, and what is at risk

## The Backlog

`content-ideas.csv` is the only source of the plan, and the calendar reads it directly. Its columns:

- `Status` is one of `Idea`, `Research`, `Script Ready`, `Made`, `Posted`
- `Post date` is `YYYY-MM-DD`, or empty for an unscheduled idea
- `Brief` is the path of the brief, `briefs/<slug>.md`, or empty until one exists

The aim is a post every Monday, Wednesday and Friday for the next two weeks. TikTok is connected read only, so posting is by hand. "Mark as posted" on the calendar asks the Script Writer to record it.

This cabinet is installed but paused by default so it behaves like a newly imported module.
