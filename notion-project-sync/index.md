# Project Sync

Your projects live in Notion databases. This puts all of them on one page, every morning.

Cabinet keeps a copy of the Notion pages you picked, task databases included, up to date on
its own. This cabinet opens on one home that Cabinet draws from that copy:

- **The one answer**: how many tasks are open, overdue and due this week, with Update a
  task, Create a task and Status this week a press away.
- **Four numbers**: open tasks, due this week, overdue, done this week.
- **Due soon** and **Overdue**, each row a task you can open.
- **By status** and **By owner**: where the work sits and who carries it.
- **Recently moved**: tasks whose status changed since yesterday.
- **Databases**: every task database Cabinet found, each a link.
- **The latest brief**: this morning's note from the Project Tracker.
- **Tools**: status this week, overdue tasks, who is overloaded, update a task, create a
  task. One press each; the answer opens in the chat beside the page.
- **Ask**: anything in your projects, in your words.

Until the first brief, the home shows example rows, clearly marked. Your own tasks replace
them.

## To start

Connect Notion from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Notion asks which pages
to share; pick the pages that hold your task or project databases. To let the Project
Tracker update a status, choose Can edit when you connect.

## What it changes in Notion

Nothing on its own. The Project Tracker changes a task's status, or adds a task, only when
you ask in the chat, and only when Notion is connected with Can edit. The morning routine
only reads.

## Inside

- **Project Tracker**: the agent. Reads your Notion databases and answers in the chat.
- **Morning projects**: the routine, every weekday at 8:30. Writes one short note on what
  moved, what is overdue, what is due this week, what is blocked, and who carries what.
- **Briefs**: one page per morning in `projects/`.
