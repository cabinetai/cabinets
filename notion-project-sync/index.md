# Project Sync

Your projects live in Notion databases. This puts all of them on one page, every morning.

Cabinet keeps a copy of the Notion pages you picked, task databases included, up to date on
its own. This cabinet opens on **This week**, a page made from that copy every morning:

- **The one answer**: how many tasks are open, overdue and due this week.
- **Four numbers**: open, due this week, overdue, done.
- **Overdue**: late tasks, oldest first, with the owner and how many days late.
- **Due this week**: what is due in the next seven days, soonest first.
- **Status**: every task, by its status in Notion.
- **Who carries what**: open and late tasks per person, the busiest first.
- **Databases**: every task database Cabinet found.

Press **Update now** to read your Notion again. Until the first reading, the page shows a
made-up team, clearly marked.

## To start

Connect Notion from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Notion asks which pages
to share; pick the pages that hold your task or project databases. To let the Project
Tracker update a status, choose Can edit when you connect.

## What it changes in Notion

Nothing on its own. The Project Tracker changes a task's status, or adds a task, only when
you ask in the chat, and only when Notion is connected with Can edit. The morning routine
only reads.

## Inside

- **Project Tracker**: the agent. Reads your Notion databases and answers in the chat.
- **This week**: the page this cabinet opens on.
- **Morning projects**: the routine, every weekday at 8:30. Updates This week and writes one
  short note on what moved, what is overdue, what is due this week, what is blocked, and who
  carries what.
- **Briefs**: one page per morning in `projects/`.
