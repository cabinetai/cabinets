# Cycle Report

Every Friday afternoon, how each team's Linear cycle is going: done against total, how much
scope was added since it started, what is at risk of slipping, and how the projects stand.

## What you get

- **The report**: the app this cabinet opens on. Each team's current cycle with a thin
  progress bar, the scope added and the issues at risk, then the projects with their health
  and latest update.
- **A page every Friday**: at 16:00 the Delivery Analyst writes `reports/<date> cycle.md`, a
  short report you can forward as it is.
- **A retro draft**: on the first Friday after a cycle ends, a draft retro beside it: what
  shipped, what slipped and why, and how much scope moved. A starting point for the meeting,
  not the meeting.

## To start

Connect Linear from the report, or from Integrations in Cabinet. Cabinet keeps a copy of your
Linear workspace, and the Delivery Analyst reads that copy. Cycles must be turned on for a
team in Linear for it to appear. Until then the report shows a made-up company, clearly
marked as an example.

## It only reads

The Delivery Analyst never creates, changes or comments on anything in Linear.

## Inside

- **Delivery Analyst**: the one agent. Reads cycles and projects, writes the report.
- **Cycle report**: the one routine. Runs at 16:00 every Friday.
- **Cycle report** (the app), with `DATA.md` describing the files it reads.

Cabinet is not affiliated with Linear.
