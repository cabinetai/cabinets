# Traffic Watch

Most mornings your website is fine. This cabinet tells you when it is not: one ranked board
of what needs a look, each item with what happened, the numbers against your usual, and one
next step.

## What you get

A **Watch Board**. Every morning it reads your Google Analytics and keeps one list:

- **Key events that stopped**: a contact form, a booking or an order that usually comes in
  every day and has not for two days. Often a broken form, so it goes first.
- **Drops**: fewer visitors than usual, or one source (search, email, social) bringing far
  fewer visits than the weeks before.
- **Broken pages**: people landing on a page that says it was not found, with the old
  address they used.
- **Jumps**: a page or a day with far more visitors than usual, so you can tell a good post
  from a flood of bots.
- **New sources**: a site that started sending you visitors this week.

Each item is ranked **Urgent**, **Worth a look** or **Can wait**, keeps its place from day
to day, and moves to **Back to normal** when the numbers settle. A quiet morning says so in
one line.

## How it works

- **It reads every morning at 8:30**, the last days up to yesterday against your own usual,
  so a small site and a busy one are both measured fairly.
- **It only reads.** Cabinet connects to Google Analytics to read only: nothing here changes
  a setting, a report, a key event or who can see your data.
- **For the week as a whole**, visitors, top pages and where they came from, see Website
  Digest.

## Getting started

1. On the board, press **Connect Google Analytics** and sign in with the Google account that
   sees your website in Google Analytics.
2. The board fills on its first check, right away when you press **Check now**.

Until then, the board shows a made-up bakery, and says so.

## What's inside

- **Traffic Watcher** is the one agent. Reads your Google Analytics and keeps the board.
- **Daily Traffic Watch** is the one routine. Runs at 08:30 every day.
- **Watch Board** is the one page. Open it to see what needs a look.
