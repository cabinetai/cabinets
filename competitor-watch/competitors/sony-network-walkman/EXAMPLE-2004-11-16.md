# Sony Network Walkman — Snapshot 2004-11-16

> **This is the shipped example** — a period-accurate reconstruction of November 2004, written so you can see the report shape.

**Surfaces checked:** positioning · pricing · product · news · retail
**One-line state:** Gorgeous hardware still handcuffed to ATRAC; the NW-HD3 announcement is close and the MP3-support question hangs over everything.

## Positioning
- **Tagline:** "Walkman. Redefined."
- **Value prop:** The thinnest 20GB player with a 30-hour battery, from the brand your parents trusted — **for** the mainstream buyer who walks into a store saying "Walkman" when they mean "MP3 player."

## Product & features
- 20GB 1.8" drive in a case smaller than the 4G iPod; class-leading 30-hour battery.
- SonicStage 2.x required for transfer; every MP3 transcoded to ATRAC3plus on sync.
- Connect store: ATRAC-only downloads, ~700K-track catalogue, no iTunes-style $0.99 uniformity in EU.
