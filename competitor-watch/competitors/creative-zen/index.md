# Creative Zen

> "It's the MP3 war, and Apple fired the first shot." — Sim Wong Hoo, November 2004

**Homepage:** creative.com · **Key line:** Zen Micro $249 (5GB) · Zen Touch $269 (20GB) · MuVo flash line · **Tracked surfaces:** positioning · pricing · product · news · social · retail

## Who they are

Creative Technology of Singapore — the Sound Blaster company — has made the iPod its declared enemy. CEO Sim Wong Hoo announced a **US$100M marketing war chest** and a target of shipping the most MP3 players in the world. The weapon is the **Zen Micro**: 5GB, ten colours, removable battery, FM radio, $249 — aimed squarely at the iPod mini, spec for spec and dollar for dollar.

- **Scale:** ~$100M pledged to marketing; claims #2 share in US hard-disk players; huge retail push for the holidays.
- **Ecosystem:** PlaysForSure / WMA side of the market — works with Napster, MSN Music, not iTunes.

## Where the iPod differs

- **One product story vs. a catalogue.** Creative ships a dozen SKUs; the iPod is one family everyone can name.
- **iTunes + iPod as one system** vs. player-plus-whichever-store-you-find.
- **Design as the product.** The Zen Micro's spec sheet beats iPod mini on paper (battery, FM, price-per-GB); the click wheel and the white earbuds are what people actually buy.

## What to watch

- **The $100M campaign** — where the money lands (TV, retail, bundles) and whether "the MP3 war" framing sticks.
- **Zen Micro sell-through** over the 2004 holidays; any price cut below $249.
- **Flash players** — Creative's MuVo line already outsells its HDD players; watch for a flash push where Apple has no product *(the iPod shuffle answer is rumoured for January)*.
