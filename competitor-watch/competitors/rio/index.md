# Rio

> The oldest name in MP3 players, with the one device reviewers keep calling "thinner than an iPod mini."

**Homepage:** rioaudio.com · **Key line:** Rio Carbon $249 (5GB) · Rio Karma 20GB · Cali flash line · **Tracked surfaces:** positioning · pricing · product · news

## Who they are

Rio shipped the famous PMP300 back in 1998 and survived the lawsuit that created this market. Now owned by D&M Holdings (Digital Networks North America), its 2004 champion is the **Rio Carbon**: 5GB Seagate microdrive, ~20-hour battery, genuinely thinner and lighter than the iPod mini, $249. The Karma remains the enthusiast's darling (gapless playback, Ogg Vorbis, ethernet dock).

- **Scale:** a veteran brand punching above its weight; strong reviews, thin marketing budget.
- **Ecosystem:** WMA/PlaysForSure side; drag-and-drop friendly.

## Where the iPod differs

- **Ecosystem gravity.** The Carbon is arguably better hardware than iPod mini; it has no iTunes, no store gravity, no halo.
- **Marketing muscle.** Rio wins reviews; the iPod wins Christmas.
- **Accessory universe** — cases, docks, car kits exist for iPod in the hundreds; Rio has a handful.

## What to watch

- **Carbon price and capacity moves** for the holidays (a $199 Carbon would be a real problem for iPod mini).
- **D&M's commitment** — persistent doubt about how long the parent will fund a loss-making player line.
- **Enthusiast features spreading** — if gapless/Ogg-class features reach mainstream rivals, the Karma's niche erodes.
