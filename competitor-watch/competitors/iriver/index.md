# iRiver

> The spec-sheet champion: colour screens, FM, recording, every codec — and now MSN Music's launch partner.

**Homepage:** iriver.com · **Key line:** H320 $329 (20GB, colour) · H140 $399 (40GB) · iFP-899 $299 (1GB flash) · **Tracked surfaces:** positioning · pricing · product · news

## Who they are

Korea's ReignCom ships players that win every feature comparison: the **H300** series has a colour screen, USB host, FM tuner and line-in recording months before the iPod Photo; the **iFP flash line** owns the gym crowd with 40-hour AA-battery life. In October iRiver became a headline hardware partner for **MSN Music**, planting it firmly in Microsoft's PlaysForSure camp for the holidays.

- **Scale:** #1 or #2 in Korea/Japan flash segments; growing US retail presence (Best Buy, CompUSA).
- **Ecosystem:** WMA/PlaysForSure + MSN Music co-marketing.

## Where the iPod differs

- **Editing the feature list vs. printing it.** iRiver ships everything; the iPod ships what matters and wins.
- **Software.** iRiver's firmware and sync tools are engineer-grade; iTunes is consumer-grade.
- **The iPod Photo** (October) just neutralised iRiver's colour-screen bragging right at the high end.

## What to watch

- **MSN Music co-marketing** — how hard Microsoft pushes iRiver hardware in holiday MSN promos.
- **Flash pricing** — the 1GB iFP-899 at $299 is exposed the moment Apple enters flash *(shuffle rumours again)*.
- **H10 line** — the rumoured 5GB colour microdrive player aimed straight at iPod mini, expected early 2005.
