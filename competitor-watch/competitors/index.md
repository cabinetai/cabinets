# Competitors

One folder per rival. Each folder holds:

- **`index.md`** — the durable profile: who they are, where your product differs, what to watch. The scout reads it before every scan and you should keep it honest.
- **Dated reports** — `YYYY-MM-DD.md` snapshots the scout writes each morning. The archive of past reviews; diff any two to see a period's story.

## The example roster (October–November 2004, seen from the iPod team)

| Folder | Competitor | One line |
|---|---|---|
| [[creative-zen]] | Creative Zen / NOMAD | The self-declared challenger — $100M pledged to "the MP3 war" |
| [[sony-network-walkman]] | Sony Network Walkman | The Walkman brand bets the comeback on ATRAC and the Connect store |
| [[rio]] | Rio | The Carbon undercuts iPod mini on thickness and battery |
| [[dell-dj]] | Dell DJ | Price and the Dell direct channel, not design |
| [[iriver]] | iRiver | Spec-sheet king, now carrying MSN Music's flag |

These folders and their `EXAMPLE-` reports are the shipped demonstration. The **Make it yours** prompt on the cabinet's front page replaces them with your real market.
