# Dell DJ

> Not a better player — a cheaper one, on the world's biggest direct channel.

**Homepage:** dell.com · **Key line:** DJ 15 $199 · DJ 20 $249 · Pocket DJ (5GB) $199 · **Tracked surfaces:** positioning · pricing · product · news

## Who they are

Dell treats music players the way it treats PCs: commodity hardware (built on Creative reference designs), aggressive pricing, and the dell.com order page as the shelf. The **DJ 20** undercuts the 20GB iPod by ~$50; the new **Pocket DJ** undercuts the iPod mini by $50 at the same 5GB. Battery life is strong, design is forgettable, and every Dell PC ships with a coupon.

- **Scale:** whatever Dell decides — the channel is the strategy.
- **Ecosystem:** WMA/PlaysForSure; bundles Musicmatch.

## Where the iPod differs

- **Desire vs. logistics.** Nobody unwraps a Dell DJ and photographs it; the iPod is a gift object.
- **Retail presence.** The DJ barely exists in stores; the iPod owns end-caps at every big-box chain.
- **Price is the DJ's only story** — and Dell can always cut deeper. Watch that, not the hardware.

## What to watch

- **Holiday price cuts and bundle coupons** — Dell moves prices weekly, silently, on the order page.
- **Pocket DJ push** against iPod mini ($199 vs $249 is a real gap on a gift budget).
- **Whether Dell stays in the category** — margins this thin get product lines killed at Dell.
