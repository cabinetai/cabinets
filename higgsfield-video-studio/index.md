# Higgsfield Video Studio

Describe a short clip and get a real video file in your cabinet.

## What you get

A page with a box on it. Write what you want to see, press Send, and a minute or two
later the clip is sitting in this cabinet as a real file you can drag anywhere. Beside
each clip is a small text file holding the exact words that made it, the model that made
it, and what it cost — so you can run it again with one thing changed.

One clip per request, about five seconds long. That is the whole thing.

## This one spends real money

Every clip costs real credits from your own Higgsfield plan — the same balance the
Higgsfield website spends. There is no separate free allowance.

Video is expensive. A single short clip can cost more than a hundred still images, and a
new account's free trial starts with 10 credits. So this cabinet makes **one clip per
request and stops at 2 credits a run**, and it never makes a second version because the
first could be better. Deciding a clip is not right is your call, and another one is what
that decision costs.

There is also a routine that makes one clip a week, and **it ships switched off**. A
routine that spends money on a timer should be switched on by the person paying. Turn it
on yourself when you want it.

## How to look at it

Open the **Studio** app in this cabinet. It comes pre-filled with example clips so the
page is not empty — it says on the page that they are examples. Your first real clip
replaces them.

## Connect Higgsfield

1. In Cabinet, open **Integrations**, find **Higgsfield**, and click **Connect**.
2. Click **Open Higgsfield sign-in**. Higgsfield opens in a new tab.
3. Sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft, or email, then approve access.

There is nothing to paste.

Sign in with the Higgsfield account that holds your credits. A different login — another
Google account, say — creates a brand-new free account. The connection will look fine,
but your clips land in the wrong place and your credits are not there.

The sign-in link expires after about five minutes. If it fails, click **Open Higgsfield
sign-in** again for a fresh one.

## What's inside

- **Video Maker** — the one agent. Turns your words into a clip and writes down what it
  cost.
- **Make a clip now** — what the Send button runs. Never runs on a timer.
- **Weekly Clip** — the one routine. Off until you switch it on; Monday at 09:00 once
  you do.
- **Studio** — the one page. Write a description, watch the clip appear.
