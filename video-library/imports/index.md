# Imports

Your bulk imports live here.

**TikTok favorites.** TikTok does not share them with apps, so ask TikTok for your data: in the
TikTok app go to Settings and privacy, Account, Download your data, and choose JSON. It can
take days. When the file is ready, download it, put it in this folder (`user_data_tiktok.json`,
or the .zip it comes in), and ask "Bring in my TikTok favorites". The agent reads only the
video links from your Favorite and Like lists, asks once, then saves them one by one. You can
delete the file once the videos are in.

**YouTube likes.** Connect YouTube, then ask "Bring in my YouTube likes". There is no file to
put here.

After each import the agent leaves a note here, named by its date, with what was saved, what
was skipped and what failed.
