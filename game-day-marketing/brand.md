# Brand

> Example brand guide. Replace with your own. The Social Creator follows this page for every post, Story and caption.

## Voice
Borough Slice sounds like the guy behind the counter who has seen every game since 2009. Quick, loud, a little funny, always on your side. Short lines. Say the neighborhood. Celebrate with fans, never trash the other team.

## Writing rules
- Lead with the moment ("Rangers took it 4-2."), then the offer, then the deadline.
- One call to action per post.
- At most two emoji per caption. No em-dashes.
- Words we like: slice, fold, the Garden, pre-game, last night, on us, grab.
- Words we avoid: official, partner, exclusive, insane, game-changer, cheap.

## Visual rules
- Brand red `#B3261E`, marigold `#F4B400`, ink `#1C1B1F`, cream `#FFF8EC`.
- Team colors (above) may be used as flat bands or stripes to signal the team. Never logos, wordmarks, mascots or jerseys with numbers.
- Big blocky display type (Impact stack) for scores and offers, clean sans for small text.
- The slice is the hero: a big, slightly cartoon slice with cheese drips.
- Fonts must be the stacks above. Ad images are exported to PNG in the browser, which cannot load web fonts.
