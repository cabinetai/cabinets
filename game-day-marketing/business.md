# Business

Borough Slice is a fictional New York pizza shop on 8th Avenue, two blocks from Madison Square Garden and around the corner from Penn Station. Classic thin-crust slices, whole pies for groups, garlic knots that disappear by 7 pm on game nights. Most customers are commuters at lunch and fans before and after games.

## How the week works
- **Lunch (11:30 to 14:00)** is office workers and commuters: fast slices to go.
- **Game nights** at the Garden bring a rush from about 17:30 until puck drop or tip-off, and a smaller one after the game.
- **The morning after a win** is the easiest crowd of the year. People want to keep celebrating.

## Goals this season
1. Turn game nights into pre-orders, so the line moves and nobody leaves without food.
2. Make "the morning after a win" a habit with the Win Slice.
3. Grow the local following on Instagram with posts people actually share.
4. Never run out mid-promotion.

## What we won't do

- No score or result we have not verified.
- No offer we cannot serve. If stock is short, we cap it. If we are out, we hold it.
- No prices that are not on the [[menu]] or in [[promotions]].
- Nothing posts without a human saying yes.
