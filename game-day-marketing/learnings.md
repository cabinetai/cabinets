# Learnings

What worked, measured against real sales. The Promo Manager adds a row after each campaign ends, using [[store-data/index]].

> The rows below come from the fictional sample sales data. They are replaced as soon as real campaigns run.

## Standing lessons
- **Pre-orders beat the line.** Game nights with the Slice Pack averaged 94 packs and about $5,380 in sales, against about $3,630 on a normal weekday.
- **The morning after a win is real.** The one Win Slice morning in the sample sold 196 of 200 slices before noon, and the whole day beat a normal weekday by about 55%.
