# Team Drive

Your shared Drive folder, able to say what changed this week and where the current
version is.

## What you get

Two pages and two people.

**Where things are** is a map of the folder. One line per folder saying what it holds,
and for the things that come in versions, which file is the current one. It is the page
you open when somebody asks "where do we keep the signed contracts".

**This week** is a short page written every Monday morning. What was added, what was
changed, what was moved or renamed, and what that means. You read it in a minute and you
know what your colleagues did without asking any of them.

Between the two, you can just ask. Type "where is the latest pricing deck" or "what
changed in Finance last week" into the cabinet and the answer comes from the folder
itself, not from a search box.

## How to look at it

The Drive folder sits in the sidebar of this cabinet, exactly where you put it. Open it
and you are looking at your real files, the same ones the Drive app keeps on this
computer. Nothing is copied anywhere.

**Where things are** and **This week** are pages in this cabinet. They come pre-filled
with a made-up example so you can see the shape straight away. Both say clearly that the
files in them are invented. Your first real run replaces them.

## Before it can read your real folder

Connect Google Drive when Cabinet asks, or later from Integrations, and pick the shared
folder you want. You do it once. It uses the Google Drive app on this computer, so
nothing new gets installed and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your team reads. It does not write to Drive, rename anything, or move a file. Until you
connect a folder, both pages keep showing the example and the two routines wait.

## If the pages still show the example

The routines only write when they can actually read a folder. If one has run and the
example is still sitting there, no Drive folder is in this cabinet yet. Nothing was
overwritten and nothing errored. Add the folder and run it again.

## What is inside

- **Drive Librarian** and **Week Watcher**. One keeps the map, one writes Monday's page.
- **Monthly Drive Map**, on the first of the month. **Monday Drive Brief**, Mondays at
  08:00.
- **Where things are** and **This week**, the two pages above.
