# Gmail for Creators

A brand desk for your inbox. Every morning at 7 it reads your email and shows:

- **Brand deals**: every offer still waiting for your answer, with the money on the table,
  how long they have waited, and their deadline. It checks each offer against your rate
  and points out the traps: usage rights, long exclusivity, late payment terms, no fee at all.
- **Owed to you**: invoices brands have not paid yet, with the late ones first and a polite
  reminder ready.
- **Looks fake**: login traps, fake brands writing from a Gmail address, "registration fees"
  and blue tick sellers, caught before you click.
- **Fans and collabs**: what your fans keep asking (each one is a video idea), and the
  creators and press who wrote to you.

On top: one big number, the money in offers waiting for your answer, and the one reply to
write first. Each reply is ready in your voice, in English or Hinglish, with your choices
(a price, a date) marked so you can change them before you send.

## To start

Open the **Brand desk** and press **Connect Gmail**. Until then it shows an example inbox
of a travel creator, clearly marked.

Tell it your rates in the chat ("my reel rate is ₹1,50,000"), or write them in **Your rates
and how you work**. Offers are checked against them.

## It never sends

Your brand desk only reads. A reply goes into your Gmail drafts only when you press
**Put in Gmail drafts**, and you send it yourself.
