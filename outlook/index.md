# Outlook

Your Outlook day on one page, every weekday at 7:

- **The day as a line of time**: your meetings, and the free stretches between them.
- **Before each meeting**: the one thing to know, taken from your mail ("Ravi sent the
  forecast last night").
- **Replies that fit**: each free stretch says which answers fit in it and how long they
  take, so the mail gets done between meetings, not after them.
- **Waiting on you**: everyone who waits for your answer, with a reply idea in your voice.

Newsletters and automatic notices are set aside and counted, never deleted.

## To start

Open **My day** and press **Connect Outlook**. Sign in with Microsoft, with a work or a
personal account. Connect **Outlook Calendar** too and your meetings join the page.
Until then the page shows a made-up day, clearly marked.

For now Outlook works in the Cabinet app on your computer.

## It only reads

It never sends, moves or deletes anything in Outlook. When you want a reply written, it
goes into your Outlook Drafts, and you send it yourself.

## Inside

- **My day**: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about your work.
- **Reports**: one page per morning.
