Every peg is one coin flip: go right with probability p, else left. Each ball's bin = its number of right turns — a sum of independent Bernoulli trials. The Central Limit Theorem guarantees that sum piles up into a bell curve. The amber outline is the exact binomial prediction C(n,k)·pk(1−p)n−k; watch the bars climb to meet it.
