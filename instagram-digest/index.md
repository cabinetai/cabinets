# Instagram Digest

Every morning, how your Instagram posts and reels did, the comments waiting for you, and what
people keep asking.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your Instagram**.

## What you get

- **Waiting for an answer**: the real questions under your posts that you have not answered
  yet, the newest first, each with a reply drafted in one tap. Nothing is posted until you
  say so.
- **How your posts did**: views, likes, comments, saves and shares for each recent post and
  reel, what changed since yesterday, and which one did best and which one was quietest.
- **Account numbers**: views, people reached, interactions and new followers for the last 7
  and 28 days, what people did (likes, comments, saves, shares, taps on your profile link),
  when your followers are online and where they live.
- **What people keep asking**: the same question asked by different people under different
  posts, counted once.
- **Ideas for your next posts**: three ideas a day, each backed by what people asked or what
  did well.
- **Latest comments**: the newest comments on your account, who wrote them and under which
  post.
- **Ask about your Instagram**: how a post did, what people said, which posts get saved. The
  answer comes up in the chat.
- **Keep more of your Instagram**: Instagram gives apps no saved posts, likes or messages,
  so a box shows how to ask Instagram for your own copy, with a picture of each screen. Put it
  in the cabinet's `imports` folder and ask "Bring in my Instagram export": your saved posts and
  likes come in as pages (comments and messages only if you ask for them).
- **Ideas**: draft replies to everyone waiting, a caption with hashtags for your next post, a
  weekly summary. Ask once, switch one on to run on a schedule, or keep it on the page as a box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** Likes and comments come from Instagram every morning. Views, reach,
saves and shares come from Instagram's numbers (insights), up to two days late. Instagram
shares follower numbers (new followers, when they are online, where they live) only once an
account has 100 followers. Until a number is there, the page says "not there yet"; it never
guesses.

## Getting started

1. Instagram works with a **Business or Creator** account. Switching is free in the Instagram
   app: Settings and activity, then Account type and tools.
2. On the page, press **Connect Instagram** and sign in on Instagram. It takes about a minute,
   once.
3. A few minutes later your own account is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up account, and says so.

## It reads, and replies only when you ask

The morning catch-up only reads. Your team replies to a comment, or hides one, only when you
ask in the chat and only if you chose Allow comments when you connected Instagram. You see the
exact words first, and nothing happens until you say yes. Everywhere else, replies are drafted
in the chat for you to post yourself. Cabinet never posts, never publishes a story or a reel,
never sends a message, and never deletes a comment.

## Bring in your saved posts and likes

1. In Instagram: Settings, Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your
   information. Pick your Instagram account, JSON, and all time or the dates you want.
2. Instagram emails you when the file is ready, as a zip. Download it.
3. Put the zip, or the folder it unzips to, in this cabinet's `imports` folder (on Cloud,
   upload it in Files), and ask **Bring in my Instagram export**.

You see how many saved posts and likes there are before anything is written. Nothing is
uploaded or sent anywhere, and a saved reel can be kept as a video by pasting its link into
Video Library.

## What's inside

- **Account Reader** is the one agent. Reads your account and writes the page.
- **Morning Instagram Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your Instagram** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
