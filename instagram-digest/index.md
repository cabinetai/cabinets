# Instagram Digest

Every morning, the questions waiting under your Instagram posts, what got saved and shared, and
what to post next.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your Instagram**.

## What you get

- **Waiting for your answer**: the real questions under your posts from the last two weeks that
  nobody answered, each one tap from a draft reply.
- **Your account**: followers, following and posts against the read before, followers as a line,
  and Instagram's numbers over 7 and 28 days.
- **How your posts did**: saves, shares, views, likes and comments for each recent post, what
  changed since yesterday, which one moved most and which was quietest.
- **Ideas for your next posts**: three a day, each from a post or a question.
- **What works for you**: saves and shares per person reached, by format, posting day and posting
  time. It shows your own posting history, not when your audience is online.
- **What people keep asking** and **Latest comments**.
- **Keep more of your Instagram**: how to bring in your saved posts and likes from Instagram's own
  copy of your data.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to suggest
  them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** Every number is counted from what Instagram sends, never by the agent.
Instagram's account numbers can be two days late. It shares no followers-by-day history and no
hours when followers are online, so the followers line builds from Cabinet's own morning reads.
From 100 followers it also sends follows, unfollows and where followers live.

## Getting started

You need a **Business or Creator** account: Instagram lets apps read only those. Switching is free
in the Instagram app: Settings and activity, then Account type and tools.

1. On the page, press **Connect Instagram** and sign in. It comes back to Cabinet by itself, about
   a minute, once.
2. A few minutes later your own account is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up account, and says so.

## It reads, and replies only after your yes

The morning catch-up only reads. In a chat, and only if you chose comments when you connected, your
team replies to a comment or hides one when you ask: you see the exact words first, and nothing
happens until you say yes. Choose read only and comments stay out of the page. Cabinet never posts,
sends a message or deletes a comment.

## Bring in your saved posts and likes

1. In Instagram: Settings, Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your
   information. Pick your Instagram account, JSON, and the dates you want.
2. Instagram emails you when the file is ready, as a zip. Download it.
3. Put the zip in this cabinet's `imports` folder (on Cloud, upload it in Files), and ask **Bring in
   my Instagram export**.

You see how many items there are before anything is written. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

## What's inside

- **Account Reader** is the one agent. Reads your account and writes the page.
- **Morning Instagram Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your Instagram** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
- **About your Instagram** (`your-instagram/setup.md`) is what you tell your reader.
