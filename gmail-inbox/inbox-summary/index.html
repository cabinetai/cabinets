3When it runs

Two routines fill this page. Both live on the agent’s Schedule tab.

  • Morning Inbox Summary, every day at 07:00, writes the six cards.
  • Friday Loose Ends, Fridays at 16:00, checks two weeks back for anyone still waiting on you. Its list shows in the Waiting card until the next morning.
  • Change a time or switch one off. The strip at the top of this page reads the real job file, so it always shows the truth. Press ▷ next to a routine to run it once, right now.
  • Each run adds one dated file, into daily-summaries/ or loose-ends/. Nothing is overwritten, so earlier days stay readable.
4What it can and can’t do
  • Reads your mail and writes this page.
  • Writes replies for you to copy. It never creates Gmail drafts.
  • Sends only when you ask. Ask your teammate in a chat to send a reply; Cabinet shows you the email and nothing goes out until you approve it.
  • Never archives, labels, deletes or unsubscribes.
  • If it cannot reach Gmail it says so and writes nothing, rather than filling the gap with a guess.
