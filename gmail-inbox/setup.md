# Your setup

Replace the answers below with your own. Plain sentences are perfect. Your teammate reads
this page before every summary, so a change tonight shapes tomorrow morning's page.

**People whose mail always matters:** Dana, my accountant, anyone writing from the
kids' school. (Their mail always gets its own row, and it goes to the top.)

**What to skip:** Order updates for things that arrived fine, LinkedIn, sale emails from
shops. (Skipped mail is left out of the page entirely. Newsletters still get counted at
the bottom, so you can decide which to unsubscribe from.)

**What counts as waiting on me:** A real person asked me a question or for a decision
and I haven't answered in two days. Group emails where I'm only copied don't count.
(This decides the "Waiting on your reply" card, every morning and in the Friday check.)

**Tone for replies:** Short and friendly. First names. Sign off with just my name, no
"Best regards". (Used for the replies written for you. Nothing is sent until you
approve it.)
