# Notes Library

Turns years of Apple Notes into a short shelf of topics you can read and ask about.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your Library**.

## What you get

- **Topics, not a pile.** The things your notes keep coming back to, a move, the car, a
  project at work, each pulled together into one page: what it is, what you decided, and
  what is still open. Between five and twenty of them.
- **A tidy-up list.** Notes that look like the same note twice, and notes with nothing in
  them, so you can clean up Apple Notes yourself.
- **Ask your notes.** What you decided about something, where you wrote it down, what you
  never got back to. The answer comes up in the chat.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Bring in your notes**. Your Mac asks once whether Cabinet may read
   Notes. Say yes, and every note is copied into Cabinet.
2. The library builds straight away, which can take a while with many notes. After that it
   rebuilds every Monday at 10.

Until then, the page shows a made-up library, and says so.

## Good to know

- **It's a copy, not a live link.** Notes you write later show up after you press **Bring
  in fresh notes** again.
- **It writes only into Topics.** The topic pages live in this cabinet's `topics` folder,
  and each rebuild rewrites them. Your notes are never changed, in Cabinet or in Apple
  Notes, and nothing is ever deleted from Apple Notes.
- **Only on a Mac.** Apple Notes can only be brought in on a Mac with the Cabinet app.
  Locked notes are skipped.

## What's inside

- **Notes Librarian** is the one agent. Reads your notes and writes the topics.
- **Build The Library** is the one routine. Runs Monday at 10:00.
- **Your Library** is the page the cabinet opens on. **Topics** holds the pages it writes.
