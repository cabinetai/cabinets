# Gmail for Personal

Your own inbox, calm. Every morning at 7 it pulls out:

- **People to answer**: family, friends, school and the people you deal with, with a
  short reply ready in your voice.
- **Bills and money**: what is due and when, subscriptions that renew, and letters from
  the bank.
- **Coming up**: appointments, bookings and dates from your email, by day.
- **On the way**: packages and orders, and when they arrive.

Newsletters, ads and promotions are set aside and counted, never deleted. The **Ideas** on
the page go further: find a warranty or a booking, see what you pay for every month, or
which newsletters you never read. Each one is a tap, now or on a schedule.

For a business inbox, use **Gmail for Business** and **Gmail for Finance**.

## To start

Open **My inbox** and press **Connect Gmail**. It takes a minute or two, and Cabinet shows
you each step. Until then the page shows a made-up person, clearly marked.

For now Gmail works in the Cabinet app on your computer. It can't be connected on Cabinet
Cloud yet.

## It only reads

It never sends, deletes or moves anything in Gmail. When you want to answer, it writes the
reply in the chat, and nothing goes out without your OK.

## Inside

- **My inbox**: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about yourself.
- **Reports**: one page per morning, and the lists you ask for.
