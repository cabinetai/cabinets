# Notes To-Dos

Every to-do you wrote down in Apple Notes, gathered on one page, the newest first.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your To-Dos**.

## What you get

- **One list instead of fifty notes.** Every checklist item, every "remember to" and every
  "call Dana on Friday", grouped by the note it came from, the notes you edited most
  recently first.
- **Coming up.** Anything with a date on it, in date order, so the thing due Thursday is
  not hiding at the bottom of a shopping list.
- **Ask about your notes.** What you promised someone, what is due this week, a short plan
  for today. The answer comes up in the chat.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Bring in your notes**. Your Mac asks once whether Cabinet may read
   Notes. Say yes, and every note is copied into Cabinet.
2. The page gathers your to-dos straight away. After that it looks again every Monday at 9.

Until then, the page shows made-up notes, and says so.

## Good to know

- **It's a copy, not a live link.** Cabinet copies your notes when you bring them in. A note
  you write tomorrow shows up after you press **Bring in fresh notes** again.
- **Ticked boxes.** Apple Notes doesn't tell Cabinet which boxes you ticked. A finished item
  can still show until you delete it from the note, or until the note says it's done.
- **Only on a Mac.** Apple Notes can only be brought in on a Mac with the Cabinet app.
- **It only reads.** Nothing is changed in Apple Notes, and your notes in Cabinet stay as
  they are. Locked notes are skipped.

## What's inside

- **To-Do Finder** is the one agent. Reads your notes and writes the page.
- **Gather My To-Dos** is the one routine. Runs Monday at 09:00.
- **Your To-Dos** is the page the cabinet opens on.
