# Zoom Meetings

Zoom keeps your meetings in Zoom. This keeps them with your work.

Every meeting you host becomes a page, with its recording's transcript and chat and its AI
Companion summary beside it, kept up to date by Cabinet. This cabinet opens on one home:

- **The next meeting**: when, how long, how soon, and Join. Nothing scheduled shows the room
  you host from instead, with Start and Copy link.
- **Start a meeting now** and **Schedule a meeting** make the meeting in Zoom and open its page.
- **Upcoming** by day, **Recent meetings**, **Recordings** and **Summaries**, each row a page.
- **Tools**: your meetings this week, transcribe a recording, summarise your last meeting,
  make tasks from next steps, who you meet with most. One press each; the answer opens in the
  chat beside the page.
- **Ask**: anything in your meetings, in your words.

Until Zoom has sent something, the home shows example rows, clearly marked. Your own meetings
replace them.

## To start

Connect Zoom from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Zoom asks you to allow
Cabinet to see your meetings and recordings; the first copy takes a minute. A Basic Zoom plan
has no cloud recording and no summaries; the home says so and the meetings still arrive.

## It only reads, unless you press

Nothing here changes a meeting in Zoom. Start a meeting now and Schedule a meeting make one
because you pressed them; everything else is read.

## Inside

- **Meeting Reader**: the agent. Reads the pages Zoom keeps here and answers in the chat.
- **Morning meeting notes**: the routine, every weekday at 8. Writes one short note on what
  happened yesterday and what is on today.
- **Notes**: one page per morning.
