# WhatsApp Catch-Up

Your customer line on WhatsApp: which customers are waiting for an answer and for how
long, the quotes and appointments agreed in chats, and the promises you made.

Open **Today** to see it. Every morning at 7:30 Chat Keeper reads your chats again.
Press **Draft a reply** on a customer and Chat Keeper writes an answer for you to check.
Nothing is sent until you say so.

## Getting started

1. In **Today**, press **Connect WhatsApp** and scan the code with your phone, the way
   you would for WhatsApp Web.
2. Pick the chats this cabinet may read: your customers and suppliers. Only those are
   saved here, in **whatsapp**.
3. Press **Catch me up now**, or wait for the morning.

## Ideas to try

Today has a row of ideas you can ask once or switch on for every week, for example
"List every customer who asked for a quote", "Customers I haven't answered in 24 hours"
and "Build my customer list from my chats".

## Good to know

- WhatsApp doesn't officially support linking Cabinet this way, so keep it to your own
  chats. Lots of automatic messages can get a number blocked. Cabinet isn't responsible
  if that happens.
- WhatsApp links to Cabinet on your computer. It can't be linked from Cabinet in the
  cloud.
- The people in the chats you pick are not told that Cabinet reads them.
- Tell Chat Keeper what matters and what to skip ("from now on, skip the family group"),
  and it keeps that in your preferences.
- Want appointments on your calendar? Connect Google Calendar too, then ask "Put the
  appointments from my chats on my calendar."
