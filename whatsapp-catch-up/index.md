# WhatsApp Catch-Up

See who is waiting for your reply, what you promised and the plans you made, from the
WhatsApp chats you pick.

Open **Today** to see it. Every morning at 7:30 Chat Keeper reads your chats again. Tap
someone who is waiting and Chat Keeper writes a reply for you to check. Nothing is sent
until you say so.

## Getting started

1. In **Today**, press **Connect WhatsApp** and scan the code with your phone, the way
   you would for WhatsApp Web.
2. Pick the chats this cabinet may read. Only those are saved here, in **whatsapp**.
3. Press **Catch me up now**, or wait for the morning.

## Good to know

- WhatsApp doesn't officially support linking Cabinet this way, so keep it to your own
  chats. Lots of automatic messages can get a number blocked. Cabinet isn't responsible
  if that happens.
- WhatsApp links to Cabinet on your computer. It can't be linked from Cabinet in the
  cloud.
- The people in the chats you pick are not told that Cabinet reads them.
- Tell Chat Keeper what matters and what to skip ("from now on, skip the school parents
  group"), and it keeps that in your preferences.
- Want plans on your calendar? Connect Google Calendar too, then ask "Put the plans from
  my chats on my calendar."
