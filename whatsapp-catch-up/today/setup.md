# Your preferences

Chat Keeper reads this page before every catch-up and every answer. Change the answers
below in your own words, or just tell Chat Keeper in the chat and it changes this page
for you.

## Chats that always matter

The building committee and the plumber. (These come first, even on a quiet day.)

## Chats to skip

The school parents group and Sunday football. (These never show up.)

## What counts as waiting for me

A question, a price or a time someone needs an answer to. A chat whose last message only
says thanks or ok is not waiting for me.

## How replies should sound

Short and friendly, in the language the chat is written in. No sign-off.
