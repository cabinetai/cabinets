# Your setup

Chat Keeper reads this page before every catch-up and every answer. Replace the answers
below with your own, in plain sentences. What you change tonight shapes tomorrow
morning's page.

## Chats that always matter

The building committee and the plumber. (These come first in every card, even on a quiet
day.)

## Chats to skip

The school parents group and Sunday football. (These never appear on the page.)

## What counts as waiting on me

A question, a price or a time someone needs an answer to. A chat whose last message only
says thanks or ok is not waiting on me.

## Tone for replies

Short and friendly, in the language the chat is written in. No sign-off.
