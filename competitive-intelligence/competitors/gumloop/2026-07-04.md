# Gumloop — Baseline Scan (2026-07-04)

First scan for the newly rostered competitor. All facts below verified against live sources on 2026-07-04; sources linked inline.

## Positioning

- Tagline: **"Build AI agents for work"** — "Understanding a task should be the only prerequisite to automating it."
- Platform for building/deploying AI agents without vendor lock-in (model-flexible), aimed at enterprise and mid-market teams automating repetitive workflows across GTM, marketing, support, and recruiting.
- Highlights: 50+ integrations (Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Linear, Asana), multi-agent orchestration canvas, scheduled/background agents, workplace surfaces (Slack, Teams, Gmail; WhatsApp "coming soon"), SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, RBAC, VPC deployment.
- Source: [gumloop.com](https://www.gumloop.com) (homepage, 2026-07-04)

## Pricing (credit model)

| Tier | Price | Credits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5,000/mo | 1 seat, 1 active trigger, 2 concurrent runs, 5 concurrent agent interactions, forum support |
| Pro | from $37/mo | 20,000+/mo (usage-selected) | Unlimited seats, 5 concurrent runs, 25 concurrent agent interactions, agent reflections, unlimited teams, unified billing, Bring-Your-Own API keys |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Pro + RBAC, SCIM/SAML, admin dashboard, usage analytics, audit logs, custom retention, security reports, VPC, workflow queuing |

- Credit model: usage-based — buyers select monthly credit volume; Pro starts at $37/mo for 20k credits and scales up.
- Coverage note: the pricing page does **not** publicly detail credit cost per action/model, nor the full Pro credit-volume price ladder. Not recorded — would need an in-app quote or archived scrape.
- Source: [gumloop.com/pricing](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing) (2026-07-04)

## Funding

- **Series A: $17M** (announced Jan 2025), led by Nexus Venture Partners, with First Round Capital, Y Combinator, and angels; total raised at that point ~$20M incl. seed. Company was ~2 FTEs + interns/contractors at the time. Sources: [Gumloop blog](https://www.gumloop.com/blog/gumloops-17m-series-a), [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/work-automation-platform-gumloop-raises-24-5-million-series-a-as-it-relocates-to-silicon-valley/) (BetaKit reports CAD $24.5M — same round, currency difference).
- **Series B: $50M** (announced 2026-03-12), led by **Benchmark**, with Nexus VP, First Round Capital, Y Combinator, Box Group, The Cannon Project, Shopify Ventures. Valuation **not disclosed** in the announcement. Named customers: Shopify, Ramp, Gusto, Samsara, Instacart, Opendoor. Source: [Gumloop blog](https://www.gumloop.com/blog/series-b).
- Stated ambition (via [PitchBook coverage](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/gumloop-1b-startup-ai-10-employees)): reach $1B valuation with ~10 employees.
- Coverage note: no reliable public valuation figure for either round; third-party ARR estimates (e.g. Latka) are unverified — excluded.
