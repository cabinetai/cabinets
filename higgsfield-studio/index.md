# Higgsfield Studio

A fresh set of images each week, made on your own Higgsfield account.

## What you get

You write a short brief — what you want pictures of, the mood, where they will be used, how
many. Once a week the routine reads it, makes exactly that many images on your Higgsfield
account, and adds a page to the gallery: the pictures themselves, the prompt behind each
one, which model made it, and what each one cost.

**It makes what the brief says and nothing else.** No spares, no "here's another version
you might prefer", no quietly trying the prompt six ways to get a better one. Three images
is the default. Six is the hard ceiling, whatever your brief asks for.

## This one spends real money

Every other routine in this collection only reads. This one **generates**, and each picture
costs real credits off your own Higgsfield plan — the same balance the Higgsfield website
spends. There is no separate free allowance for agents.

Roughly: the cheapest image model is about **0.15 credits a picture**, so a normal run of
three costs about half a credit. A new account's free trial starts with 10 credits. Video
is a different story — a single short clip can cost more than a hundred images — so the
routine never makes video unless your brief asks for it in plain words.

Whatever the brief says, a single run stops at **2 credits**. Every set the routine writes
puts the total it spent right at the top of the page, in credits.

## This routine is off until you switch it on

**This routine is off until you switch it on, because every run spends real Higgsfield
credits.** Nothing happens — no images, no charges — until you go to the routine and turn
it on yourself. Everything else here ships ready to go; this one waits for you on purpose.

## Write your brief

Open **brief.md** in this cabinet and edit it like a note to a person. It comes filled in
with a made-up bakery so you can see the shape of it: what to make, the mood, where it goes,
how many. Replace all of it with yours.

While the example bakery is still sitting there, the routine makes nothing and tells you
why — so an untouched cabinet can never spend a credit by accident.

## How to look at it

Open the **Gallery** app in this cabinet. It comes pre-filled with a made-up example set so
you can see the shape straight away, and the page says clearly that those pictures are not
real. Your first real set takes its place.

## Before it can use your real Higgsfield

Connect Higgsfield in Cabinet's integrations screen: click Connect, then sign in in the
Higgsfield tab that opens — Google, Apple, Microsoft or email — and approve it. Nothing to
paste.

**Sign in with the account that actually holds your credits.** Picking a different login —
another Google account, say — quietly creates a brand-new free account. The connection will
look perfectly fine, but your images land in the wrong place and your credits are not there.

The sign-in link expires after about five minutes. If it fails, click Connect again for a
fresh one and go through it a bit quicker.

## What's inside

- **Image Maker** — the one agent. Reads your brief, makes the images, writes down what
  each one cost.
- **Weekly Image Set** — the one routine. Monday at 09:00, once you switch it on.
- **Gallery** — the one page. The newest set, with a dropdown for earlier ones.
- **brief.md** — what you want made, in your own words.
