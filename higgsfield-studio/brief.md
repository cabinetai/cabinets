# Brief

This file is the whole control panel. Whatever it says here is what gets made — nothing
else, and nothing extra. Write it in your own words, the way you would describe the job to
a person. There is no right format and nothing to look up.

**The four lines below are a made-up example.** Replace all of them before you switch the
routine on. While the example is still here, the routine makes nothing and tells you why.

---

## What to make

Photographs of sourdough loaves and pastries on a scrubbed wooden counter, shot from just
above, with one window's worth of daylight coming in from the left.

## Mood

Warm, calm, a bit old-fashioned. Flour dust in the air. No people, no hands, no text in the
picture.

## Where it goes

The front page of the Fern & Loaf bakery website, and the odd Instagram post.

## How many

3

---

## About "How many"

Three is the default, and three is usually right — it gives you a choice without spending
much. **Six is the hard ceiling.** Asking for more than six changes nothing: you get six.

Each picture costs real credits from your own Higgsfield plan. At the cheapest image model
that is roughly 0.15 credits each, so three images is about half a credit. The whole run
stops at 2 credits no matter what this file asks for.

## Video

Leave video out unless you really want it. **A video clip costs many times what an image
costs** — a single short clip can cost more than a hundred images. If you do want one, say
the word "video" here in plain English and say how long. Anything less explicit than that
and you get pictures only.
