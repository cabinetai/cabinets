# Phone Line

Your business phone line in one place. Who is waiting for a text back and for how long,
the calls to return, what callers asked for, and the appointments and prices that came up.

The cabinet opens on it: **Phone line**.

## What you get

- **Who is waiting**: everyone who texted and has no answer yet, longest first, with what
  they want in a few words.
- **Texts**: every conversation, with search and filters (Waiting for you, Customers,
  All). Open one and Cabinet's draft is already in the reply box. Change it, press Send.
- **Calls**: missed calls to return, voicemails with what was said, and a line on each call.
- **Appointments and quotes** that came up in texts and calls.
- **Ideas**: plays like "Text everyone who missed a call today". Ask once, or switch one on
  for every morning or every Monday.

## Getting started

1. In Phone line, press **Connect Twilio**. You copy two keys from Twilio's first page and
   paste them. It takes about 3 minutes, once.
2. Your texts and calls show up within a minute. Every morning at 7, Front Desk reads the
   new ones: names, what people want, and reply drafts.

Until then, the app shows a made-up dental office, and says so.

## Good to know

- You need a Twilio account with a phone number. Twilio charges for its number (about a
  dollar a month) and for each text and call (about a cent a text).
- Nothing is sent without you. The morning read only writes drafts; a text goes out when
  you press Send, or when you say yes in the chat.
- Cabinet reads calls, it doesn't answer them. Calls that come in show who called and
  whether anyone picked up. A voicemail or a recorded call shows what was said when your
  Twilio number records it.
- Twilio works in Cabinet on your computer. It can't be connected in Cabinet in the cloud.
- Tell Front Desk who a number is ("the number ending 0133 is our parts supplier") and it
  keeps that in your preferences.
