# Your preferences

Front Desk reads this page before every morning read and every answer. Change the answers
below in your own words, or just tell Front Desk in the chat and it changes this page for
you.

## Who is who

Numbers you want named. (One per line, for example: (515) 555-0133 is Keystone Supply, our
parts supplier.)

## Numbers to skip

None yet. (Texts and calls from these never show up as waiting.)

## What counts as waiting for me

A question, a price or a time a customer needs an answer to. A text that only says thanks,
ok or confirms is not waiting for me.

## How replies should sound

Short, friendly and professional. First names. Sign off with the business name when it is
someone new.
