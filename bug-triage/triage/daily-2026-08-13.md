   use `(severity × 3) + (log10(occurrences+1) × 20) + (customers × 2)`. The Engineering
   Agent persona specifies `(severity × 3) + (occurrence_log × 2) + (customers × 1)`.
   These rank bugs differently — the job formula weights raw occurrence volume ~10×
   heavier. One of them has to win before any score is trustworthy.
