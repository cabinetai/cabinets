# X Digest

X tells you about every like, follow and bot. This shows the people who wrote to you and want
an answer, with a reply ready to post.

Every morning at 9, your digest reads what was aimed at you on X since the last morning and
opens on one page:

- **The morning note**: a post from Cabinet with the one thing that matters, and every mention
  as a small dot through the night, the ones that want you in blue.
- **Waiting on you**: people who replied to you, quoted you or asked you something, and that
  you have not answered yet, oldest wait first. Each one shows their post exactly as X shows
  it, what they want in one line, and a reply in your voice. Edit it, then press **Reply on X**:
  X opens with your reply filled in, and you press Post.
- **Your posts**: your newest posts with X's counts, and which one still has people waiting.
- **The numbers**: how many mentions you already answered, and what this reading cost from
  your X credits.

Press **Layout** to make the page yours: add or remove boxes by hand (drag a box to move
it, pull its corner to resize it, **Add a box** for more), or ask the agent and press Apply.
Every box uses what the morning already read, so adding one costs nothing more: **Your posts**
(which one got people talking), **Quoted you**, **Who talks to you most**, **Safe to skip**
(bots, giveaways and "gm", counted), **Your next 3 replies**, and the numbers.

Ask anything about your X in the box at the bottom, or open **Ideas** ("Thank everyone who
quoted me", "Questions I never answered this week") and ask once or set it up as a routine.

## To start

Open **Catch up** and press **Connect X**. Cabinet uses your own X app: the connect window
walks you through making one, adding a few dollars of credits and copying its key, in about
five minutes. Then press **Read my X now**. The first reading looks at the last week and shows
you what it finds as it reads. Until then the page shows a made-up account, clearly marked.

## What it costs

X charges your X app for every post it reads, about half a cent a post. A usual morning costs
a few cents. Each reading reads at most fifty mentions, and it stops at the spending limit you
set in X's Developer Console.

## It only reads

It never posts, replies, likes, reposts, follows or sends a message. X allows replies written
with AI only when you post them yourself, so every reply opens on X for you to post.

## Inside

- **Catch up**: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about your X.
- **X Reader**: the agent. Reads your X, writes the page, answers in the chat.
- **Morning X Digest**: the routine, every day at 9.
