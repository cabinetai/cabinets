# News Desk

Your news sites, read every morning — and analyzed the way you would, not the way a
wire feed would.

## What you get

At 06:30 the analyst reads the sites you've listed and writes one edition: the three
to six stories that actually matter by your rules, each in two parts — **what
happened**, in plain facts, and **your angle**, the story read through the lens you
described in setup. Facts and analysis never blur: the angle is always labelled as
the angle.

No infinite feed, no fifteen tabs. One page, and at the bottom a line per site
saying what was read — so a thin morning is thin news, never a broken run.

## Make it yours

Open `setup.md` and replace the example answers — the sites you trust, the topics you
follow, the lens you read through, and what to skip entirely. This is the whole
product: the same routine with your answers becomes *your* desk. The analyst reads it
before every edition, so tonight's correction shapes tomorrow's page.

## How to look at it

Open the **Edition** app in this cabinet. It starts pre-filled with a made-up example
so you can see the shape — the page says plainly that those stories are fake. Your
first real morning takes its place.

## What's inside

- **News Analyst** — the one agent. Reads your sites, writes your edition.
- **Morning Edition** — the one routine. Runs at 06:30 every day.
- **Edition** — the one page. Opens on today, with a dropdown for earlier days.
- **setup.md** — your sites, your topics, your lens.
