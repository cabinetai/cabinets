# Discord Feedback

Your community tells you what to build, in Discord, every day. This cabinet reads your
`#feedback` channel, and any message someone marks with a 📌, and keeps one board of what
people ask for: each request once, how many people asked, what kind it is, and how much it
matters.

## What you get

A **Feedback Board**. Similar messages land on the same row, so "dark mode please",
"need a dark theme" and "+1 dark mode" are one request asked by three people, not three
lines. Chatter, greetings and thank-yous are left out. Every row keeps the people who asked
and the messages themselves, so you can always check the source.

## How it reads

- **It reads `#feedback`**, every 30 minutes, and only what is new since the last time.
- **A 📌 sends any message to the board.** React with 📌 on a message in any channel you
  let Cabinet read, and it is on the board after the next update.
- **It reads, and writes only where you allow.** The board itself only reads, and nothing
  writes in Discord on its own. In the chat, your team posts, replies or reacts only when you
  ask, only in channels you set to Write in Discord's settings in Cabinet, and after you see
  the words. Everywhere else, replies are drafted for you to send.
- **One server**: the one you added Cabinet to.

## Getting started

1. On the board, press **Connect Discord**, or connect it from Integrations in Cabinet. You
   add Cabinet to your server and choose which channels it may read, `#feedback` among them.
2. The board fills on its next update, or right away when you press **Update the board**.

Until then, the board shows a made-up example, and says so.

## What's inside

- **Feedback Analyst** is the one agent. Reads new messages and keeps the board.
- **Organize Feedback** is the one routine. Runs every 30 minutes.
- **Feedback Board** is the one page. Open it to see the board.
