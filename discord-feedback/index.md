# Discord Feedback

Your community tells you what to build, in Discord, every day. This cabinet finds the
channels where people ask for things and keeps one board of what they want: each request
once, how many people asked, who they are, and links to their own messages.

## What you get

A **Feedback Board**. Similar messages land on the same row, so "dark mode please", "need a
dark theme" and "+1 dark mode" are one request asked by three people, not three lines.
Chatter, greetings and thank-yous are left out. The same request stays one row from one
month to the next, and two rows that turn out to be the same thing are merged.

From any row you can draft a reply for the thread, write a spec as a page in this cabinet, or
say the request shipped.

## How it reads

- **It finds your feedback channels.** Any channel you let Cabinet read that is named for
  feedback, suggestions, ideas, feature requests or bugs, forum channels included. The board
  says which ones it used, and you can add or leave out a channel by asking in the chat.
- **No channel like that?** It looks through your general channels instead, says so, and
  tells you how to point it at the right one.
- **Three times a day**, at 9:00, 13:00 and 17:00, and only what is new since the last check.
  The first check goes back up to 90 days, about 600 messages, and says how far it reached.
- **Feedback somewhere else?** Paste a link to the message in the chat beside the board, and
  it is added.
- **It only reads.** Nothing is written in Discord on its own. In the chat, your team posts
  only in a channel set to Write, after you see the exact words and say yes. Cabinet's bot
  posts under its own name, and it never pings anyone.
- **One server**: the one you added Cabinet to, and only the channels you chose.

## Getting started

1. On the board, press **Connect Discord**, or connect it from Integrations in Cabinet. You
   add Cabinet's bot to your server and choose the channels it may read.
2. The first check starts by itself and takes a few minutes.

Until then, the board shows a made-up example, and says so.

## What's inside

- **Feedback Analyst** is the one agent. Reads new messages and keeps the board.
- **Organize Feedback** is the one routine. Runs at 9:00, 13:00 and 17:00.
- **Feedback Board** is the one page. Open it to see the board.
- **specs/** is where a spec goes when you ask for one.
