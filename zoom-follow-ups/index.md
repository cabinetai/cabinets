# Zoom Follow-ups

A meeting ends and the next steps are already on their way: as tasks in Cabinet, and as a
short note ready to send to everyone who was there.

This cabinet opens on one home:

- **Waiting for a follow-up**: the meetings that ended and have no note yet, newest first,
  each with its next steps counted.
- **Make tasks from next steps**: one press on the latest summary makes one task per step,
  each linking back to the summary.
- **Notes**: the follow-up notes written so far, one page per meeting, with the draft to copy.
- **Ask**: what you promised, who is waiting on you, what was decided.

## To start

Connect Zoom from Integrations in Cabinet, or when Cabinet asks. Summaries with next steps
need a licensed Zoom plan with AI Companion; without them, the notes are written from the
transcript when there is a recording, and from the meeting page otherwise.

## It only reads

Nothing is sent from here. Every note is a draft on a page, yours to copy into mail or chat.

## Inside

- **Follow-up Writer**: the agent. Reads a meeting's summary or transcript and writes its note.
- **After each meeting**: the routine, every hour on weekdays. Writes the note for every meeting
  that ended since the last run.
- **Notes**: `follow-ups/`, one page per meeting.
