# Real Estate Map

Every property you own as a pin on a map. Hover a pin for the facts. Press it for the
state, the numbers, the dates and anything else you keep about the place.

## How it works

The **Map** app is where you live. Each pin is one property, coloured by its state:
rented, vacant, your own use, renovating, for sale or sold.

- **Hover** a pin to read everything known about it without opening it.
- **Press** a pin to open the property. Change its state with one press. Type straight
  into any number or date. Keep a to-do list and notes.
- **Add a field** for anything you want to keep: a mortgage, a tenant, an insurance
  renewal, a gate code. There are about fifty to pick from, and you can make your own.
- **Add a property** by pressing the map, by searching its address, or by telling your
  assistant.

Beside the map is your board. It starts simple: your numbers, your list and a place to
ask. **Edit layout** lets you remove anything on the page, the map included, and add
more boxes: reminders, what is coming up, the rent roll, the mix, a goal, notes. **Ideas** holds one-press jobs for your assistants.

Two assistants keep it true:

- The **Portfolio Manager** answers questions about the whole portfolio, checks values
  and rents, and writes the summaries. On the first of each month it sends you a short
  review.
- The **Property Keeper** adds and updates properties from what you tell it, watches
  every date, and drafts the notes to tenants. Every Monday it tells you what is coming
  up. Nothing is ever sent for you.

## Where things are kept

A property an assistant adds is a file in `properties/`. What you add or change on the
map is kept by the map itself. For one property the newer of the two always shows.
`playbooks/how-the-map-works.md` has the details.

The seven properties you see at first are examples, made up, in and around Austin, Texas.
Press **Remove them** on the board when you have seen enough.
