# YouTube Digest

Every morning, how your YouTube channel did, the comments waiting for you, and what viewers
keep asking.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your YouTube**.

## What you get

- **Waiting for an answer**: the real questions under your videos that nobody from the
  channel answered yet, the newest first, each with a reply drafted in one tap. Nothing is
  posted until you say so.
- **How your videos did**: views, likes and comments for each recent video, what changed
  since yesterday, and which one did best and which one was quietest.
- **Channel numbers**: views, watch time, how long a view lasts, subscribers gained and lost,
  where viewers came from and their countries, for the last 7 and 28 days.
- **What viewers keep asking**: the same question asked by different people under different
  videos, counted once, so you see what your audience wants to know.
- **Ideas for your next videos**: three ideas a day, each one backed by what viewers asked
  or what did well.
- **Latest comments**: the newest comments on your channel, who wrote them and under which
  video.
- **Ask about your channel**: how a video did, what people said about it, who asked about a
  topic. The answer comes up in the chat.
- **Ideas**: draft replies to everyone waiting, a weekly summary, better titles for quiet
  videos. Ask once, switch one on to run on a schedule, or keep it on the page as a box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** Views, likes and comments come from YouTube for every channel. Watch
time, where viewers came from and their countries come from YouTube Analytics, about two days
late, and only once you allow YouTube's Analytics permission. Until then the page shows what
it has and says so; it never guesses.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect YouTube** and sign in with the Google account that owns the
   channel. It takes about a minute, once.
2. A few minutes later your own channel is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up channel, and says so.

## It reads, and replies only when you ask

The morning catch-up only reads. Your team replies to a comment, or changes a video's title
or description, only when you ask in the chat and only if you chose Allow comments when
you connected YouTube. You see the exact words first. Everywhere else, replies are drafted in
the chat for you to post yourself. Cabinet never uploads or deletes a video, never changes
who can see one, and never removes a comment.

## What's inside

- **Channel Reader** is the one agent. Reads your channel and writes the page.
- **Morning YouTube Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your YouTube** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
