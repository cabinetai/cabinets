# YouTube Digest

Your channel, read for you every morning at 8. The **Your channel** page shows:

- **This week**: views, watch time and new subscribers, next to the week before, with a
  bar for each of the last 28 days.
- **Waiting for your answer**: comments nobody answered yet, real questions first, each
  with a reply already written in your voice.
- **Your latest videos**: how each one is doing next to your usual video.
- **What viewers keep asking** and **ideas for your next video**, taken from your own
  comments and numbers.

## To start

Open **Your channel** and press **Connect YouTube**. Cabinet shows you each step. Until
then the page shows a made-up channel, clearly marked as an example.

## Nothing goes out without you

The morning read only reads. When you press **Send this reply**, the reply shows up as a
card in the chat with the exact words. It is posted on YouTube only after you approve the
card, and you can change the words first.

## Inside

- **Your channel**: the page this cabinet opens on.
- **Reports**: one short page per morning, and anything you ask for in the chat.
