# Website Digest

Every morning, how your website did this week, where visitors came from, and what changed.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your website**.

## What you get

- **The week in one line**: how many people visited in the last 7 days against the 7
  before, your key events, and the biggest change, with a small chart of the last 28 days.
- **What changed since last week**: the few things that moved most, in plain words. "Google
  search brought 28% more visits", "Cake inquiries went from 5 to 12".
- **Key events**: the things you want visitors to do, like sending a form or placing an
  order, as you marked them in Google Analytics, against last week.
- **Site numbers**: visitors, page views, engaged visits, key events or sales, for 7 or 28
  days, with countries and phones against computers.
- **Top pages** and **where visitors came from**: search, social media, other websites,
  email and ads, each against last week.
- **Ask about your website**: how a page did, why a day was busy, how many people are on
  the site right now. The answer comes up in the chat.
- **Ideas**: a weekly summary, the pages worth a fix, a monthly report to share. Ask once,
  switch one on to run on a schedule, or keep it on the page as a box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**Real numbers only.** Every number comes from Google Analytics as Google gives it, for the
days it covers, which end yesterday because today's numbers are still coming in. The page
never guesses.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect Google Analytics** and sign in with the Google account that
   sees your website in Google Analytics. It takes about a minute, once.
2. A minute or two later your own website is there. It updates every morning at 8.

Until then, the page shows a made-up bakery, and says so.

If your account has more than one website, the page follows the busiest one and says how
many others there are. To follow another, ask in the chat.

## It only reads

Cabinet connects to Google Analytics to read only. Nothing here changes a setting, a report,
a key event, or who can see your data, and Cabinet never asks Google for that permission.

## What's inside

- **Site Reader** is the one agent. Reads your website's numbers and writes the page.
- **Morning Website Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 08:00 every day.
- **Your website** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
