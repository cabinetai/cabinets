# Slack Digest

Every morning, what moved in each of your Slack channels, the ones that need you first.

The cabinet opens on it: **Your Slack**.

## What you get

- **Waiting on you**: the questions and mentions nobody answered yet, the oldest first, each
  with a reply drafted in one tap. Nothing is posted until you say so.
- **Catch up by channel**: one line per channel that moved, the ones that need you first. The
  sixty-message thread about lunch sits at the bottom.
- **Latest messages, decisions and files**: the newest messages, what the team agreed, and
  the files shared, each in its own box.
- **Ask about your Slack**: who mentioned you, what a channel talked about, what the team
  decided. The answer comes up in the chat.
- **Ideas**: draft your standup from Slack, keep the week's decisions on one page, a weekly
  summary for your manager. Ask once, switch one on to run on a schedule, or keep it on the
  page as a box.
- **Your page, your way**: move, resize, add and remove the boxes, or ask the agent to
  suggest them. Each person's page is their own.

**All of your channels, not a chosen few.** You pick channels once when you connect Slack,
and every public channel is ticked by default, so the quiet one you forgot you were in
still turns up on the morning it needs you.

**Your direct messages too.** They count in the catch-up, and the page never quotes them.
To leave them out, open the routine and change `Include direct messages: yes` to `no`.

## Getting started

1. On the page, press **Connect Slack**. Cabinet shows you each step in Slack's own
   console, about two minutes, once.
2. A few minutes later your own Slack is there. It updates every morning at 9.

Until then, the page shows a made-up team, and says so.

## It reads, and writes only where you allow

The morning catch-up only reads, and nothing writes in Slack on its own. Your team writes in
Slack only when you ask for it in the chat, and only in channels you set to Write in Slack's
settings in Cabinet. You see the exact words first, and they are posted after you say yes.
Everywhere else, replies and updates are drafted in the chat for you to send yourself. Cabinet keeps your Slack keys in a locked file on your
own machine, and writes them down only after the sign-in has actually worked.

## What's inside

- **Channel Reader** is the one agent. Reads your Slack and writes the page.
- **Morning Slack Digest** is the one routine. Runs at 09:00 every day.
- **Your Slack** is the page the cabinet opens on, with earlier days in its date picker.
