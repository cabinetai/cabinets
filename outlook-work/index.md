# Outlook for Work

Your workday from Outlook on one board, every weekday at 7:

- **The board**: today's meetings like a departures board, with the one that is on now,
  the next one and when it starts, and a Join button for Teams.
- **Before your meetings**: the one thing to know before each meeting, taken from your
  mail ("Ravi sent the forecast last night").
- **Waiting on you**: everyone who waits for your answer, your manager and your team
  first, each with a reply drafted in your voice.
- **Your day**: the free stretches between meetings, and which replies fit in each.
- **You promised**: what you told people you would do, and by when.
- **Invites to answer**: meetings you have not said yes or no to.

Newsletters, alerts and all-company mail are set aside and counted, never deleted.

## To start

Open **My workday** and press **Connect Outlook**. Sign in with Microsoft, with your work
account. Connect **Outlook Calendar** too and your meetings join the board. Until then
the page shows a made-up workday, clearly marked.

For now Outlook works in the Cabinet app on your computer.

## Make it yours

- **Ideas**: one tap for things like a weekly status update for your manager, or getting
  ready for your next meeting. Any idea can also run by itself on a schedule.
- **Layout**: add, remove, move and resize the boxes, or tell the agent what you need and
  it suggests the boxes.
- **Ask**: anything about your mail and calendar, in plain words.

## It only reads

It never sends, moves or deletes anything in Outlook. When you want a reply written, it
goes into your Outlook Drafts, and you send it yourself.

## Inside

- **My workday**: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about your work.
- **Reports**: one page per morning.
